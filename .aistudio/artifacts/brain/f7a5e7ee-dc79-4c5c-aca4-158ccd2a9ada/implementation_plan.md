### 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Provides a complete, self-contained 17-chapter (60-unit) Sanskrit academy and reference book. Learners can study every unit interactively—with audio pronunciation, Pāṇinian derivation tools, and quizzes—or read, search, and download the complete multi-hundred-page **Sanskritam — Complete Curriculum PDF** containing all 17 chapters.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Students of Sanskrit, Vedic chanters, linguistics & computer science enthusiasts exploring Pāṇini, philosophy readers, and educators needing full content export for video production or classroom study.
- **Key Value**: Bridges the gap between beginner Varnamala lessons and genuine scholarly mastery (Vedic hymns, philosophical śāstras, scientific treatises, and Pāṇini's *Dhātupāṭha*/*Gaṇapāṭha*) in both an interactive web sandbox and a typeset PDF book.

### 2. User Experience & Visual Design

- **Key User Flows**:
  1. **Complete 17-Chapter Guided Syllabus**: Users browse all 17 chapters and 60 units in the right-hand syllabus navigator, filter by topic or unit number, and step through every lesson and exercise with live audio and instant verification.
  2. **Advanced Chapter Deep-Dives (Chapters 13–17)**: Users explore dedicated interactive exercises for Vedic *Sūktas*, *Darśana* syllogisms, *Navarasa* aesthetics, *Kaṭapayādi* scientific number encoding, and the 10 *Dhātupāṭha* verb classes (*Daśagaṇī*).
  3. **In-App PDF Viewer & Export Studio**: Users click the **Curriculum PDF** navigation link to open the interactive PDF reader/viewer, jump to any chapter (Chapters 1–17), inspect the formatted book pages (matching the exact typography and✓ CORRECT key markers of the original document), or download the updated `Sanskritam-Curriculum.pdf` file directly.
- **Visual Identity & Theme**:
  - *Aesthetic Direction*: Scholarly museum editorial meets modern interactive educational sandbox.
  - *Color Palette & Mood*:
    - **60% Dominant Neutral Canvas**: Crisp warm off-white (`#F8FAFC`) for effortless reading of dense Devanagari and IAST diacritics.
    - **30% Structural Surfaces**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards with `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline borders, paired with deep slate (`#0F172A`) analytical consoles for vocal tract, sūtra, and prosody visualizations.
    - **10% Semantic Focal Accents**: Vibrant Azure (`#0284C7`) for active interactive states and audio playback, Emerald (`#059669`) with explicit `✓ CORRECT` / `● NOMINAL` labels for verified answers, and Amber (`#D97706`) for *इत्*-markers and review prompts.
  - *Typography & Hierarchy*:
    - **Display & Classical Headings**: `Fraunces` paired with `Noto Serif Devanagari` (`clamp(1.75rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)`).
    - **Body & Bilingual Prose**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` + `Noto Serif Devanagari` (`15px–16px`, `1.6` line-height) for crisp Hindi, Sanskrit, and English legibility.
    - **Algebraic Sūtras & Tabular Metrics**: `IBM Plex Mono` (`tabular-nums`) for Pāṇinian sūtra indices (`6.1.77`), *Dhātupāṭha* class numbers, and page/exercise counters.
  - *Component Styling & Layout*: Strict 3-zone Top Bar Contract, Two-Zone Educational Sandbox (`67%` interactive stage / `33%` syllabus & control deck), zero static pill badges (all metadata rendered as clean inline text with `·` separators), and single-elevation depth.
