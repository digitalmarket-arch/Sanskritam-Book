# Research notes — Volume 1, Chapter 4 (अन्तःस्थाः ऊष्माणश्च — Semivowels & Sibilants)

Verified facts and word inventories gathered **before** drafting v1.c04. Chapter 4 delivers
the final 8 consonants — the four antaḥsthā (य र ल व) and the four ūṣman (श ष स ह) — and
with them the **complete varṇamālā**. The readable-vocabulary unlock is the largest of the
volume: र brings राम and गुरु; स brings सीता and भाषा; ह completes हिमालय and गृह; and three
new readable verbs (भवति, वदति, हसति) make the pause-form sentence set genuinely expressive.

Cumulative glyph ledger entering Ch 4: all vowels + mātrās + ं ः + ॐ + 25 stops + क म त ल
(long since full). Additions per lesson below. Every word checked against
Apte/Monier-Williams; nothing else may appear as readable vocabulary.

## 1. L1 — अन्तःस्थाः: य र ल व (ल upgraded to full)

**Readable words (attested, curated ~14 from the huge unlock):** राम (rāma) — THE word, at
last; गुरु (guru) "teacher" (course term readable — v1.c01.l01 callback); देव (deva) "god,
deity"; जय (jaya) "victory"; नर (nara) "man"; वन (vana) "forest"; वीर (vīra) "hero"; रवि
(ravi) "sun"; वायु (vāyu) "wind"; नव (nava) "new; nine" — cognate with English *new*, Latin
*novus* [REF:mallory2006] (v1.c01.l01's cognate table pays off in Devanagari); कवि (kavi)
"poet"; युग (yuga) "age, era" — the *yoke/yoga* PIE family, loan-vs-cognate refresher;
**भवति (bhavati) "is, becomes"** and **वदति (vadati) "speaks"** — verbs 2 and 3.
Also readable now, reserve for examples: रथ, तरु, यव, नयन, भय, लय, वर, करुणा, विनय, वचन,
कुमार, नारी.
**Sentences:** रामः वदति। गुरुः भवति... (keep predicates honest: रामः वदति। कविः वदति। वीरः
चलति।)

## 2. L2 — ऊष्माणः I: श ष स

**Readable words (attested):** शिव (śiva) "auspicious; Śiva" (gloss order: adjective sense
FACT, deity sense one TRADITION line); शुभ (śubha) "auspicious, good"; शत (śata) "hundred" —
cognate Latin *centum* [REF:mallory2006]; दश (daśa) "ten" — cognate *decem* (the honest
cognate framing from v1.c01.l01, NEVER "October from aṣṭa"); रस (rasa) "juice, essence,
flavor"; सुख (sukha) "happiness, ease"; सीता (sītā) "Sītā; furrow"; दास (dāsa) "servant";
मास (māsa) "month"; सेवा (sevā) "service"; ऋषि (ṛṣi) "seer" — finally readable, the /r̩/
lesson's poster word; भाषा (bhāṣā) "language, speech" — course meta-term readable; पुरुष
(puruṣa) "person, man"; दोष (doṣa) "fault"; विष (viṣa) "poison".
**Contrast drills:** श/ष at syllable level (शा-षा, शि-षि…) + विष/विश? (विश् is a root — skip;
use कोश/कोष orthographic-variant note? skip — syllable drills + word discrimination शिव/षिव*
nonce marked as such).
**Trap:** सत्य, शक्ति, दृष्टि etc. contain conjuncts — display 🔊 only, Chapter 5 promise.

## 3. L3 — ऊष्माणः II + completion: ह

**Readable words (attested):** हरि (hari) "Hari; tawny"; हर (hara) "Hara; taking away"; हल
(hala) "plough"; हित (hita) "beneficial, good"; हिम (hima) "snow, cold"; **हिमालय
(himālaya) "Himālaya — the snow-abode"** — transparent compound हिम + आलय, the chapter's
showcase word; मही (mahī) "the earth"; बाहु (bāhu) "arm"; मोह (moha) "delusion"; देह (deha)
"body"; गृह (gṛha) "house" — the 1.x book's घर confusion corrected in passing (गृह is the
Sanskrit; Hindi घर is its descendant — FACT, one line, no 1.x mention); इह (iha) "here";
**हसति (hasati) "laughs"** — verb 4; वहति (vahati) "carries, flows".
**The completion moment:** all 46 letters + 2 signs owned; the full varṇamālā recited start
to finish (the L4 assessment's centerpiece); नमस्ते status: न-म-स्-ते needs the स् virāma —
"one chapter away" (Ch 5 promise tracker).

## 4. L4 — Review & assessment: the Alphabet Exam

Mirrors the established review architecture (Station 0 = Ch 3's +1-week interval; per-lesson
stations; five tasks). Cumulative readable vocabulary ≈ 140 words. Task E = the full
varṇamālā written from memory in traditional order (vowels → ayogavāha note → 25 stops by

with a vowel" (FACT); "the varṇamālā's order is arbitrary" (reject — v1.c01.l02 callback).
