# Research notes — Volume 1, Chapter 3 (स्पर्शाः — The Stop Consonants)

Verified facts and word inventories gathered **before** drafting v1.c03. Chapter 3 delivers
the 25 stops — one lesson per varga, **all five members with equal depth** (the fix for
IR-042: the 1.x book gave letter cards only to "corner" letters). The manner row
(voiceless / voiceless-aspirated / voiced / voiced-breathy / nasal) is learned properly once
in L1 and *reapplied* four times — each later lesson gets faster because the row transfers.

Cumulative glyph ledger entering Ch 3: all 13 vowels + ॐ, all mātrās, ं ः, consonants
क म त ल. Each lesson below lists its additions and a verified readable-word set (every word
checked against Apte/Monier-Williams; nothing else may appear as readable vocabulary).

## 1. L1 — क-वर्ग (velar row): क ख ग घ ङ

New letters: ख ग घ ङ (क upgraded from helper to full treatment — its varga home).

| Claim | Register | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Velars: back of tongue + soft palate; /k kʰ g gʱ ŋ/ per contract. | FACT | STYLE_GUIDE §2.2 |
| क unaspirated — English "sky"-k, not "kite"-k (which is [kʰ]). | FACT | [REF:ladefoged2015] |
| ख the tissue-thrower; घ breathy ("murmured g"); ङ = [ŋ] of "sing", now with its own letter. | FACT | [REF:ladefoged2015] |

**Readable words (attested):** खग (khaga) "bird, sky-goer" [REF:apte1890] — the 1.x book's
verified etymology gem lands here (ख "sky" + ग "going" as *bound elements*, a real
derivational note, FACT); गत (gata) "gone"; गति (gati) "motion, way"; गीत (gīta) "song";
मुख (mukha) "mouth, face"; लेख (lekha) "writing"; लघु (laghu) "light, small"; मेघ (megha)
"cloud"; घृत (ghṛta) "ghee"; खल (khala) "wicked (person)". ङ: no common standalone-initial
words — its card teaches the shape + the अङ्क/अंक equivalence from v1.c02.l06 (spiral
callback), and recognition in अङ्क-type interiors (conjunct display only, reading deferred
to Ch 5).
