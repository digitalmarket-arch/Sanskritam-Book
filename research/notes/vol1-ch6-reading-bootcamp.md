## 1. L1 — दीर्घशब्दाः: long words & the hidden nasals

**Theme:** multi-akṣara fluency + the ङ/ञ payoff. **Readable unlock (attested):**
गङ्गा (gaṅgā) "the Gaṅgā" — ङ्ग, the ङ finally at work; अङ्गम् (aṅgam) "limb";
पञ्च (pañca) "five" — ञ्च; सङ्घः (saṅghaḥ) "assembly" (the सङ्घे spelling-audit word,
IR-audit callback); **संस्कृतम् (saṃskṛtam) — the course's own name becomes readable**
(स + ं + स्क + ृ + त + म् — anusvāra, conjunct, vowel-sign, virāma: four chapters in one
word); नमस्कारः (namaskāraḥ) "salutation" (नमस्ते's formal sibling, स्क + repha…
actually र after का is plain र — components: न म स ् क ा र ः); वर्णमाला (varṇamālā)
"alphabet" — the course term, repha ✓; देवनागरी (devanāgarī) — the script's name (etymology
debated — say so; [REF:masica1991 ch.6]); महाभारतम् (mahābhāratam); रामायणम् (rāmāyaṇam);
उपनिषद् (upaniṣad) — final द् ✓; विद्यालयः (vidyālayaḥ) "school" — transparent compound
विद्या + आलयः "abode of learning" (**Pass C: confirm Apte/MW s.v.; if thin classically,
frame honestly as standard modern-Sanskrit compound**). Stretch/display: भगवद्गीता (द्ग by
the mechanism). Drill design per 0.6: timed known-word rereading, component-count races.

## 2. L2 — संवादः: the first dialogue

**Frame:** self-introduction — the course's first true conversation. **New (attested):**
नाम (nāma) "name" — मम नाम …। "my name is …"; त्वम् (tvam) "you" — त्व by the mechanism;
तव (tava) "your"; एतत् (etat) "this"; छात्रः (chātraḥ) "student" (MW s.v. chāttra; त्र ✓
ch5); for a female speaker बालिका/बाला (attested earlier) — **Pass C: decide छात्रा
(modern usage) vs बाला; default बाला/बालिका to stay classical**. Politeness pair: त्वम्
(intimate) vs भवान् (respectful, ✓ ch5.l01) — one honest line, no paradigms.
**Dialogue script (all parts certified):**
— नमस्ते! अहम् रामः। तव नाम किम्?
— मम नाम सीता। त्वम् कः?
— अहम् छात्रः अस्मि। त्वम् अपि छात्रा वा? (अपि "also", वा "or/question-softener" —
**Pass C: वा usage; simpler alternative किम् त्वम् अपि छात्रा?** keep whichever reads
cleanest classically). एतत् किम्? एतत् पुस्तकम्। (पुस्तकम् may debut here or L3 —
drafting decides; inventory lists it in L3.)
अपि (api) "also" — अ प ि ✓ simple, attested, high-utility.

## 3. L3 — अनुच्छेदः: the first connected passage

**New (attested):** पठति (paṭhati) "reads, recites" — √पठ्, THE chapter verb (पठनम् is its
noun); पठामि "I read" (1sg parallel to वदामि ✓ ch5); पुस्तकम् (pustakam) "book";
तस्य (tasya) "his" — स्य ✓ mechanism; इति (iti) — quotation particle, glossed as "closing
quote mark spoken aloud" (**optional — Pass C may defer इति to V2 if the passage reads
better without the quoted line**). **The passage (draft skeleton, every word certified):**

> बालकः अत्र अस्ति। सः छात्रः अस्ति। तस्य नाम अर्जुनः। सः विद्यालयम् गच्छति।
> सः पुस्तकम् पठति। सः संस्कृतम् पठति। छात्रः हसति। अहम् अपि संस्कृतम् पठामि।

"He reads Sanskrit… I too read Sanskrit" — the bootcamp's meta-moment. Object forms per
policy 0.7. Comprehension questions in Sanskrit using the certified question words
(कः? किम्? कुतः?). Female-learner mirror line with बालिका. Name अर्जुनः framed neutrally
(a common Sanskrit name; epic resonance = one SYMBOL-register aside at most).

## 4. L4 — श्लोकः परीक्षा च: the first śloka & the Volume Exam

Three texts, three registers — the chapter's register-discipline finale:

1. **विद्या ददाति विनयम्** (vidyā dadāti vinayam) "knowledge gives humility" — Hitopadeśa,
   prastāvikā. First pāda only, **zero sandhi** in pause form; every word certified
   (विद्या ✓ ch5, ददाति = द द ा त ि ✓, विनयम् ✓). The learner's first line of real
   literature. [REF:hitopadesha] (new entry; **Pass C: verse number in prastāvikā**).
2. **सत्यम् एव जयते** (satyam eva jayate) — Muṇḍaka 3.1.6, shown both resolved (pause form)
   and joined (सत्यमेव — the म्+ए join previewed as "Volume 3's subject, but you can already
   see it"). HISTORY per 0.5: the national motto. एव (eva) "alone, indeed" ✓; जयते (jayate)
   "prevails" ✓ whole-word gloss.
3. **सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः…** — the full maṅgala śloka as recitation piece, TRADITION register
   with the honesty note that doubles as register teaching: *traditional benediction of
   unrecorded source — often mis-attributed to the Upaniṣads; we say "traditional" because
   that is what the evidence supports.* Pause-form reading line resolves the two sandhi
   spots: मा कश्चित् दुःखभाक् भवेत् (**Pass C: confirm pausa form भाक् of -भाज्;
   [REF:whitney1889] final-consonant rules — locate § or gloss without citing**). All
   components certified: सर्वे (repha), भवन्तु/सन्तु/पश्यन्तु (न्त, श्य), सुखिनः,
   निरामयाः, भद्राणि (द्र), कश्चित् (श्च), दुःख (mid-word visarga ✓ ch2.l06), भवेत्.
   Syllable-counting exercise delivers fact 0.4 hands-on.

**Volume Exam** (five-task format, Station 0 = ch5 +1-week interval): A varṇamālā +
mātrā sweep from memory; B conjunct two-way sample (build ∕ dissect); C reading aloud —
the milestone ladder (word list → dialogue lines → the L3 passage → विद्या ददाति विनयम्);
D dictation (words + one sentence); E the śloka: recite + count syllables + two
register-card questions. Passing = Volume 1 complete; gate to Volume 2 with an explicit
"what you can now do / what Volume 2 adds" bridge.

## 5. Pedagogy anchors

Fluency strand per 0.6: every drill uses **only known material at speed** (timed word
recognition, repeated reading of the same passage with a target time, component-count
races). Word load modest (~25–28 new across the chapter, cumulative ≈220) because the
chapter's job is automaticity, not acquisition [REF:nation2013]. Retrieval/spacing
architecture unchanged [REF:roediger2006]; [REF:cepeda2006].
