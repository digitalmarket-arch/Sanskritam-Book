## 0. Chapter-wide facts

| 0.1 | Virāma (हलन्त) ् silences the inherent vowel: क् = /k/ alone. Word-final bare consonants are written with it: अहम्, तत्. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6]; [REF:whitney1889 §9] | Law 5 of the script (Laws 1–4 in v1.c02.l07 §15). |
| 0.2 | A conjunct (संयुक्ताक्षर) writes a consonant cluster as one akṣara: the non-final consonant appears in a reduced form (usually losing its vertical stem), the final keeps its full form and carries the vowel. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | The mechanism, taught once, generates most conjuncts. |
| 0.3 | Three formation patterns: horizontal (stem-loss: स्त न्त स्क), vertical stacking (mostly ट-varga and क्क: ट्ट ङ्क), and irregular fused forms that must be learned as units (क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र द्ध…). | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | Honest taxonomy; the irregulars get their own lesson. |
| 0.4 | र has special forms: as FIRST member, repha — a hook above the following akṣara (धर्म, कर्म); as SECOND member, a diagonal stroke on the preceding consonant (प्र, क्र) with variants (ट्र). रु रू are irregular vowel-attachments (रु रू). | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | The repha "writes right, reads left" surprise parallels the ि hook (v1.c02.l01 callback). |
| 0.5 | Remaining signs: avagraha ऽ (marks an elided अ in sandhi writing — display only until V3), candrabindu ँ (nasalized vowel), daṇḍa । and double daṇḍa ॥, and the digits ०–९. | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §16]; [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | Digits taught as content per STYLE_GUIDE §3. |
