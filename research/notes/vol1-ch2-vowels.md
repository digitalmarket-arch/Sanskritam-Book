# Research notes — Volume 1, Chapter 2 (स्वराः — The Vowels)

Verified facts and word inventories gathered **before** drafting v1.c02 lessons. Chapter 2
completes the vowel system: every remaining vowel with its mātrā the same day (the
mātrā-with-vowel rule, volume.md), plus the two ayogavāha. **No new consonants** — the
readable-word inventory grows only through vowels attached to the Chapter 1 consonant set
{क म त ल}. Every word below is checked against Apte/Monier-Williams; nothing outside these
lists may appear as "readable" vocabulary in Chapter 2.

Chapter glyph ledger (cumulative): Ch 1 gave अ आ क म त ल + ा. Chapter 2 adds letters
इ ई उ ऊ ऋ (ॠ ऌ shown for completeness) ए ऐ ओ औ + mātrās ि ी ु ू ृ े ै ो ौ + signs ं ः + ॐ.

## 1. Lesson-by-lesson sound facts

### L1 — इ /i/ and ई /iː/ (mātrās ि, ी)

| # | Claim | Register | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | इ = /i/ short close front unrounded; ई = /iː/ same quality, double duration. | FACT | STYLE_GUIDE §2.1; [REF:ladefoged2015] | English "ee" (feet) is closest to ई; English short i (bit) is /ɪ/ — lax, NOT इ. State the imprecision. |
| 1.2 | The ि mātrā is written to the LEFT of its consonant although the vowel is spoken AFTER it: कि = k + i. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | The script's famous left-hook surprise — the chapter's key writing fact; drill hard. |
| 1.3 | ी attaches to the right: की. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | Symmetry with ा. |
| 1.4 | English-speaker trap: substituting lax /ɪ/ (bit) for इ; Hindi speakers largely transfer correctly but often blur इ/ई length in fast speech. | FACT | [REF:ohala1994], [REF:ladefoged2015] | Feeds §12. |

**Readable words (all attested):** इति (iti) "thus — the quoting word" [REF:apte1890 s.v. इति];
तिल (tila) "sesame" [REF:apte1890]; मति (mati) "thought, mind" [REF:apte1890] — pairs with
Ch 1's मत; कति (kati) "how many" [REF:apte1890]; कील (kīla) "pin, wedge" [REF:apte1890];
लीला (līlā) "play" [REF:apte1890]; ममता (mamatā) "mine-ness, attachment" [REF:monierwilliams1899
s.v. ममता]; मित (mita) "measured, moderate" [REF:apte1890]. Minimal pair for length:
मति/मती? — no; use **कि/की syllable pair + मित/मीत?** मीत not attested — length pair at word
level: तिल vs तील? not attested. Use syllable-level drills (कि की मि मी ति ती लि ली) +
इति/ईति? ईति (īti) "plague, calamity" IS attested [REF:apte1890 s.v. ईति] → **इति vs ईति** is
a genuine minimal pair (thus vs calamity) — verified, use it.

| 2.2 | ु and ू attach BELOW the consonant: कु कू. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] |

### L3 — ऋ /r̩/ (mātrā ृ); ॠ, ऌ for completeness

| # | Claim | Register | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | ऋ = /r̩/ syllabic r — a vowel made by holding the tongue in the r-position. Target value; modern regional realizations [ɾɪ]~[ɾi] ("ri") and [ɾu] (Maharashtra) are habits, not the classical target. | FACT | STYLE_GUIDE §2.1; [REF:whitney1889 §24] | The 1.x book taught "ri" as the value (IR-009); we teach /r̩/ with the variants noted honestly. |
| 3.2 | ॠ /r̩ː/ occurs almost only in inflection (e.g. genitive plurals); ऌ /l̩/ occurs in forms of √कॢप् only. Taught for inventory completeness, no vocabulary load. | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §§23–26] | One paragraph + recognition only. |
| 3.3 | ृ attaches below-right of the consonant: कृ मृ तृ. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | |
| 3.4 | अमृत (a-mṛta) "deathless" and Greek ámbrotos/ambrosía descend from the same PIE root *mer- "die" with negative prefix — cognates. | FACT | [REF:mallory2006], [REF:burrow2001] | The lesson's payoff: the ear-word from v1.c01.l03 becomes READABLE and gets its etymology. |
| 3.5 | मातृ (mātṛ) is the dictionary stem of माता — introduces the concept "stem vs inflected form" in one honest paragraph (full treatment Volume 3). | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §370ff], [REF:apte1890 s.v. मातृ] | Readable: मा + तृ. |

**Readable words (attested):** ऋत (ṛta) "cosmic order, truth" [REF:apte1890]; ऋतु (ṛtu)
"season" [REF:apte1890]; कृत (kṛta) "done" [REF:apte1890]; मृत (mṛta) "dead" [REF:apte1890];
मातृ (mātṛ) "mother (stem)"; अमृत (amṛta) "deathless, nectar" [REF:apte1890]. **Minimal pair:
कृत vs मृत** (initial consonant) and the triple मत / मित / मृत ("opinion/measured/dead") for
vowel discrimination. ऋत vs ऋतु for syllable count.

### L4 — ए /eː/ and ऐ /ɐi̯/ (mātrās े, ै)

| # | Claim | Register | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | ए = /eː/ — ALWAYS long, pure monophthong. No short e exists in Sanskrit. English "hey" [eɪ] is a glide — banned anchor (STYLE_GUIDE §2.3). | FACT | STYLE_GUIDE; [REF:whitney1889 §27] | Fixes IR-010 in the curriculum itself. |
| 4.2 | ऐ = /ɐi̯/ — a true diphthong, onset matching अ. | FACT | STYLE_GUIDE §2.1 | |
| 4.3 | े and ै sit ABOVE the headstroke: के कै. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | First above-the-line mātrās. |
| 4.4 | Traditional grammar classes ए ऐ ओ औ as sandhyakṣara ("junction vowels" — historically a+i, a+u fusions); this explains sandhi patterns met in Volume 3. | TRADITION + FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §§27–29], [REF:burrow2001] | One TRADITION callout; no sandhi drilling yet. |

**Readable words (attested):** एक (eka) "one" [REF:apte1890] — high-frequency payoff word;
एकता (ekatā) "oneness, unity" [REF:monierwilliams1899 s.v. एकता]; केतु (ketu) "banner, comet"
[REF:apte1890]; केलि (keli) "play, amusement" [REF:apte1890]; एला (elā) "cardamom"
[REF:apte1890]; तैल (taila) "oil" [REF:apte1890] — derived from तिल (L1's sesame!) by vṛddhi:
honest one-line preview of derivation, full treatment v4. **Contrast drill: े vs ै** in
के/कै, मे/मै, ते/तै, ले/लै syllables; word pair केलि vs कैलि? not attested — use syllable
contrasts + एक/ऐक? ऐक not standalone — syllable drills suffice.

### L5 — ओ /oː/ and औ /ɐu̯/ (mātrās ो, ौ) + ॐ

| # | Claim | Register | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | ओ = /oː/ — always long, pure monophthong (not English "go" [oʊ]). औ = /ɐu̯/ diphthong. | FACT | STYLE_GUIDE §2.1 | Fixes IR-010's second half. |
| 5.2 | ो = ा + े combined; ौ = ा + ै: को कौ. | FACT | [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | Visually compositional — easy after L4. |
| 5.3 | ॐ is a special ligature (U+0950) conventionally analyzed in the tradition as a-u-m. | TRADITION | [REF:monierwilliams1899 s.v. ओम्]; Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad tradition | The a-u-m decomposition is the tradition's analysis (Māṇḍūkya framework) — TRADITION register; its sacred senses are SYMBOL. The 1.x book's treatment (p.8, 21) was verified correct and lands here per the preservation map. |
| 5.4 | Phonetically, careful recitation of ओम् is [oːm] with a long vowel. | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §28] | FACT stays separate from the symbolism. |

**Readable words (attested):** लोक (loka) "world, people" [REF:apte1890]; कोमल (komala)
"soft, tender" [REF:apte1890]; कोकिल (kokila) "cuckoo" [REF:apte1890]; लोल (lola) "swaying,
eager" [REF:apte1890]; कौतुक (kautuka) "curiosity, festivity" [REF:apte1890]; लौकिक (laukika)
"worldly" [REF:apte1890] — derived from लोक by vṛddhi (echoes L4's तिल→तैल pattern);
ॐ (the symbol, read oṃ). **Pair: लोक/लौकिक, ओ/औ syllable ladder को कौ मो मौ तो तौ लो लौ.**

### L6 — अं and अः: the ayogavāha (ं, ः)

| # | Claim | Register | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Anusvāra ं marks a nasal element; before a stop it is pronounced as the nasal of that stop's varga (homorganic): अंक = [ɐŋkɐ]. | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §§70–71] | With only क म त ल available, demonstrate with अंक [ŋ] and अंत? त gives [n̪]: अंत not classical standalone (अन्त is) — see word list note. |
| 6.2 | Visarga ः = voiceless /h/ echo of the preceding vowel's position; extremely frequent word-finally (रामः pattern). | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §67] | Recitation echo-vowel ([ɐhɐ]) = TRADITION note (STYLE_GUIDE). |
| 6.3 | अं/अः are ayogavāha — reaffirmed from v1.c01.l02; this lesson delivers the full treatment that lesson promised. | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §§70–73] | Spiral closure. |
| 6.4 | Classical orthography often writes the homorganic nasal explicitly (अङ्क for अंक); anusvāra spelling is the common modern convention; both read identically. | FACT | [REF:whitney1889 §73], [REF:masica1991 ch.6] | Honest note; conjuncts arrive v1.c05. |

**Readable words (attested):** कः (kaḥ) "who?" [REF:apte1890] — the question word from the
l02 dialogue एषः कः वर्णः, now readable in part; ततः (tataḥ) "then, thereafter" [REF:apte1890];
अतः (ataḥ) "hence" [REF:apte1890]; इतः (itaḥ) "from here" [REF:apte1890]; कुतः (kutaḥ) "from
where?" [REF:apte1890]; मतिः (matiḥ) — L1's मति in its dictionary citation form with visarga;
अंक (aṅka) "hook; lap; mark, number" [REF:apte1890 s.v. अङ्क] with the अङ्क orthography note.
**Payoff:** the story-glue word अत्र stays 🔊 (त्र conjunct), but ततः/अतः give the learner
their first *sentence-connector* words — usable in the L7 story retell.
