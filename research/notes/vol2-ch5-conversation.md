Fact base gathered **before** drafting v2.c05 — the chapter that turns the Volume 2
toolkit (greetings, family and world, numbers and time, the present singular) into
conversations the learner can actually hold. Salvage: the 1.x Chapter 5 "Conversation"
unit (PDF pp.44–46: Lesson 5.1 question words, 5.2 responses, 5.3 shopping and travel —
words largely correct, grammar never explained, usage labels absent). 2.0 keeps the useful
core, labels every chunk honestly, and drops the 1.x bare adjective list (सुन्दर, महत्,
लघु, उत्तम printed as stems with no word on agreement). Entering ledger: 308 (v2.c04 exit,

## 5. L5 — पुनरावृत्तिः: the conversation exam (review)

Five tasks: A listening (question word → the kind of answer it wants); B dictation;
C the role-card conversation — the centerpiece: greet → introduce family → ask when/why →
want/like → buy at the market → farewell, recorded, improvised from cards (production);
D register/honesty sort — cards incl. "मह्यम् रोचते literally means 'I like'" (reject —
"it pleases me"), "कः, quis and who share one ancestor" (FACT), "Sanskrit borrowed its
question words from Latin" (reject), "अहम् न अवगच्छामि is classical grammar in a modern
classroom use" (FACT — the both-things-true card), "वदतु is the you-form of वदति"
(reject — an imperative chunk, a different mood); E the unscripted round: answer six
questions from an examiner using the nine askers. Ledger stays 328 (chapter: 20 new).
Gates v2.c06 (कथाः शब्दयात्राश्च).
