# Chapter 1 — प्रवेशः · Entering Sanskrit Sound

The door into the course. In five lessons the learner goes from zero to reading real Sanskrit
words: first an honest orientation (what Sanskrit is, what the evidence says, how this course
separates fact from tradition), then the sound system as a *map*, then the first two sounds —
अ and आ — taught to production quality with IPA and articulation, along with six Devanagari
letters, the first mātrā, and twelve readable words. The chapter models every convention the
next 139 lessons will use.

## Goals

- Orient the learner honestly: history with evidence, tradition with attribution, symbolism
  as symbolism — demonstrated, not just promised
- Install the sound map (places of articulation; the grid) as the mental model for Volume 1
- Teach अ /ɐ/ and आ /ɑː/ to production quality, with vowel length as a meaning-bearing feature
- Teach the abugida mechanics early: inherent vowel (L3) and the ā-mātrā (L4)
- Establish the lesson rhythm: review → new → practice → produce → revise

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Distinguish and produce /ɐ/ vs /ɑː/ in isolation and inside words (कल vs काल)
- [ ] Read and write अ, आ, क, म, त, ल and the mātrā ा with acceptable stroke order
- [ ] Read at least 8 real Sanskrit words aloud with correct vowel length
- [ ] Say संस्कृतम् and नमस्ते with careful pronunciation
- [ ] Explain, with one example each, the difference between a grammatical fact, a historical
      claim, a traditional interpretation, and a symbolic association about Sanskrit

## Assets manifest

All chapter assets are `planned:` at this stage (generation prompts live in each lesson's
§23–24): mouth-position diagrams (अ, आ), letter-formation animations (6 glyphs + ा), audio
drills (sounds, syllables, minimal pairs, word list, dictation set), tracing worksheets,
and the chapter quiz sheet with separate key.

## Assessment

Lesson 5 assesses every can-do above: minimal-pair discrimination (listening), dictation
(writing), reading aloud (recorded), the four-register sorting task, and a scored quiz with
separated key. Rubric in l05 §22 (Teacher Notes).
