# Chapter 5 — संयोगः · Virāma & Conjuncts

No new sounds — every sound is already certified. Chapter 5 teaches **combination**: the
virāma that silences the inherent vowel, the conjunct system that fuses clusters into single
akṣaras, the two faces of र, and the last signs of the script. This is the chapter that makes
the written language whole: अहम् becomes writable in lesson 1, नमस्ते — tracked since
Chapter 1 — in lesson 2, ज्ञान and मित्र in lesson 3, धर्म and कर्म in lesson 4. By the
chapter's end the course's every 🔊 glue word has retired, and the learner reads
**नमस्ते! अहम् अत्र अस्मि।** with nothing borrowed.

## Goals

- Virāma mechanics and final consonants (Law 5 of the script)
- The conjunct mechanism (stem-loss joining) taught once, generating the transparent set
- The irregular ligatures (क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र द्ध…) as learned units with their components visible
- Both r-forms: repha above, diagonal below — and रु रू
- The remaining signs: avagraha, candrabindu, daṇḍa, digits ०–९
- Reading fluency: component-analysis both directions (build and dissect conjuncts)

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Write bare consonants and final-consonant words (अहम्, तत्, जगत्) with virāma
- [ ] Build and dissect transparent conjuncts using the stem-loss mechanism
- [ ] Read and write the irregular ligatures क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र द्ध द्व and the r-forms
- [ ] Read 57 new words (214 cumulative) incl. नमस्ते, सत्य, ज्ञान, धर्म, कर्म
- [ ] Read the milestone line नमस्ते! अहम् अत्र अस्मि। and name every component in it
- [ ] Read and write the digits ०–९ and use daṇḍa punctuation correctly
