# Volume 1 — वर्णमाला · Sounds & Script

Volume 1 turns a complete beginner into someone who can **hear, say, read, and write every
sound of Sanskrit**. It is phonetics-first: each sound arrives with its IPA value, its
articulation, its Devanagari letter *and* its vowel-sign (mātrā), and immediately gets used
in real words. By the end, the learner reads connected Sanskrit words fluently and recites
a first śloka — with pronunciation a paṇḍita would recognize as careful.

## Entry profile

No prior Sanskrit, no Devanagari, no linguistics. Curiosity required; nothing else.

## Exit profile

- Produces all Sanskrit sounds with correct place and manner (verified against the IPA table)
- Reads and writes the full varṇamālā: vowels, consonants, mātrās, virāma, core conjuncts
- Reads real multi-syllable words and short phrases aloud, with correct vowel length
- Recites one memorized śloka; explains fact vs tradition vs symbolism about the script
- Ready for Volume 2 (vocabulary and sentences)

## Design notes

- **Mātrā-with-vowel rule:** every vowel lesson teaches its mātrā the same day (the 1.x
  curriculum never taught mātrās at all — analysis/IMPROVEMENT_REPORT.md IR-007).
- **Helper consonants:** Chapter 1 previews क म त ल (glyph + sound only) so vowels are
  practiced inside real words from day 3; the vargas get full treatment in Chapter 3
  (spiral principle, EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES §1).
- **Honest counting:** 13 vowels; anusvāra/visarga taught as ayogavāha in Chapter 2 — the
  category is stated once and used consistently (fixes IR-008).
- **Every varga member equal:** Chapter 3 gives ग घ ङ छ झ ञ ठ ड ढ थ ध न फ ब भ the same
  depth as क and प (fixes IR-042).
- Debts to later volumes: sandhi is *named* when visible (e.g. in the Ch 6 śloka) but taught
  in v3.c04; grammar terminology beyond script rules waits for v3.
