# Chapter 1 — माहेश्वराणि सूत्राणि · The Fourteen Sūtras

Volume 3 left the learner with 6.1.77 इको यणचि, quoted as the rule behind इत्यवदत् — five
syllables nobody had yet taught them to read. This chapter teaches the alphabet the grammar is
written in. The first lesson gives the fourteen sūtras, अइउण् to हल्, written into the Volume 1 grid
and recited, with the marker that closes each line (1.3.3, 1.3.9) and the pratyāhāra, a sound plus
a marker naming every sound up to it (1.1.71); with them इको यणचि reads "ik before ac: yaṇ", and the
learner derives इति + अवदत् → इत्यवदत् step by step. The 1.x book's errors in this territory are
corrected in the open, each quoted with its page: चय् is voiceless *unaspirated*, झश् is not जश्,
the memory aid gains sūtra 12, and इको यणचि is 6.1.77 and not 1.1.45; the "~42 pratyāhāras", a count
given without its method, is quoted beside the Kāśikā's own count (41 named marker by marker, with ञम् and the र of 1.1.51 noted apart), printed with its method. The Śiva-drum story stays what the 1.x book
called it, a legend, labelled and never assessed. The second lesson asks how long a letter in a rule
reaches: a short vowel stands for its whole family of savarṇas (1.1.69, with 1.1.9), a vowel
written with त् for its own length only (1.1.70) — which explains विद्यालयः and, at last, why
गच्छामि has a long आ and गच्छथ does not. The third lesson teaches the case code in which every rule
speaks: the genitive "in place of", the locative "before", the ablative "after", the nominative the
result (1.1.49, 1.1.66, 1.1.67), with 1.3.10 for pairing in order; with it the learner reads every
vowel rule of Volume 3, derives the last step of भवति (भो + अति, the first step of the volume's
भवति thread) and the -ऐः of देवैः [7.1.9], and from now on derives forms unaided. The fourth lesson
is the Chapter 1 exam, one closed-book sitting of 45 minutes, in which Volume 3's vowel joins are
re-derived, according to Pāṇini, step by step from his rules. Every quoted sūtra is printed as the
Aṣṭādhyāyī data file gives it; every commentary line is "text of the GRETIL e-text".

## Goals

- Recite, write and use the fourteen sūtras (T9), with every marker; the rebuilt memory aid with
  all fourteen
- Name any stretch of sounds by its pratyāhāra, and expand the fifteen readings the chapter opens
  (अच्, हल्, इक्, यण्, अक्, एच्, एङ्, यञ्, झल्, जश्, झश्, चय्, इण्, and अण् read two ways)
- Say how long a letter in a rule reaches: savarṇa (1.1.69 with 1.1.9), tapara (1.1.70), the two
  ण्
- Read the case code of a sūtra — genitive, locative, ablative, nominative — with the padaccheda
  printed beneath it, and tell the two अतः apart
- Re-derive, with full understanding and in table form, the owned vowel joins of v3.c04.l01 by
  kind (सवर्णदीर्घ, गुण, वृद्धि, यण्, अयादि), according to Pāṇini
- The 1.x errors corrected in the open (IR-017, IR-018), and the "~42 pratyāhāras" set beside the Kāśikā's counted method; the legend kept
  as TRADITION, never assessed (IR-036); the HISTORY of the sūtras and of Pāṇini hedged and keyed
- The Hindi parallels: the order of the Hindi वर्णमाला against the sūtras' order; दीर्घ सन्धि and
  सवर्ण; के स्थान पर / से पहले / के बाद against the bare cases of a rule

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Recite and write the fourteen sūtras from memory, marking every marker, and say where each
      line sits on the grid of places and manners
- [ ] Expand a pratyāhāra from the sūtras, sound by sound, including the traps (अण् and इण्, the
      two हs, चय् against झश् and जश्)
- [ ] Say which sounds a letter written in a rule stands for, and why, according to Pāṇini
- [ ] Name the case and the job of each word of a quoted sūtra, and say what the rule replaces, by
      what, and where
- [ ] Derive, according to Pāṇini, in table form, five owned vowel joins (नास्ति, विद्यालयः,
      सूर्योदयः, तत्रैव, इत्यवदत्), भवति from भो + अति, नयति, and देवैः, रामैः
- [ ] Say and write sentences about the sound system in Sanskrit, joined, with the pause form
      beneath
- [ ] Read 13 ledger-new words (521 cumulative; Volume 4 so far: 13)
- [ ] Pass the Chapter 1 exam in one 45-minute sitting

## Assessment

Lesson 4 is the Chapter 1 exam: one closed-book sitting of 45 minutes, 2 of them for instructions;
Station 0 (about 8 minutes) runs before it and outside it. **A** — the fourteen sūtras written from
memory, every marker circled (6 min; 14 points, pass 11). **B** — ten pratyāhāras expanded, three
of them traps: अण् as every rule but 1.1.69 reads it and as 1.1.69 reads it, against इण्; हल् from the ह of
हयवरट्; चय् against झश् — 7 min; 10, pass 7. **C** — six sūtras of the case-code table read:
what is replaced, by what and where — 6.1.77, 6.1.78, 6.1.101, 7.1.9, 7.3.101, 8.4.40 (10 min; 18,
pass 12). **D** — "Derive, according to Pāṇini, …": five owned vowel joins in table form, each step
with its sūtra — नास्ति, इत्यवदत्, तत्रैव, सूर्योदयः, विद्यालयः — 12 min; 20, pass 14. **E** — six
Sanskrit sentences about the sound system with the chapter's words, joined, with the pause form
beneath, then said aloud (8 min; 12, pass 8). Pass: every task at its pass mark, and the §19 quiz
(after the sitting) at 7/10. The rubric weights the reading and the derivations (C and D, 38 of 74
points) over recall (A, 14). After the sitting, unscored, the learner re-reads the Volume 3 thread
verse सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः… — it has no vowel join between words — and names the owned sūtras of its
two changed seams (8.2.39) and of the visarga inside कश्चित् [8.3.34, then 8.4.40]. No legend or
SYMBOL content is assessed. Retake after one week, with the alternative item sets of the Teacher
Notes. Station 0 chain: l01 runs the Volume 3 exam's +1-day booking and v3.c06.l04's +1-week
booking; l02 runs l01's +1-day item and the Volume 3 exam's +1-week booking; l03 runs l02's +1-day
item and l01's +1-week item; l04 runs l03's +1-day item and l02's +1-week item; l03 books its +1-week
item for v4.c02.l01; l04 books its +1-day item for v4.c02.l01 and its +1-week item for v4.c02.l02.
