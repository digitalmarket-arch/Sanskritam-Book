# Chapter 2 — अष्टाध्यायीप्रवेशः · Inside the Aṣṭādhyāyī

Chapter 1 taught the alphabet the grammar is written in and the case code in which its rules speak.
This chapter opens the machine those rules drive, on forms the learner has used for three volumes.
The first lesson takes रामः apart: a base (प्रकृतिः) and an affix (प्रत्ययः) placed after it (3.1.1–2),
the affix's marker named (1.3.2) and dropped (1.3.9), the base called aṅga before it (1.4.13) — and the ः of रामः, learned
in Volume 1, turns out to be what is left of the ending. Before case endings the base has two more
names, pada and bha (1.4.17, 1.4.18), except before the five strong endings (1.1.43), and what a rule does to it depends on the name: राजन् becomes
राजभिः as a pada and राज्ञा as a bha, and the 1.x book's rājan card is corrected in the open, its
errors quoted with their page. नमः comes from नमस् by the same tools, and the स् of नमस्ते proves to be
a substitute for the visarga. The second lesson shows that a rule does one of three things: it
replaces (आदेशः), adds (आगमः) or deletes (लोपः); guṇa and vṛddhi, the Volume 3 names of two vowel joins,
are Pāṇini's names for sets of substitutes (1.1.1–3). अगच्छत् is derived as all three operations at
once, and the 1.x claim that in भवति "गुण inserts व" is corrected: two substitutes, nothing inserted.
The third lesson reads rules that do not say everything they mean: words carried down from earlier
rules (अनुवृत्तिः) and headings that stand over a stretch (अधिकारः), on the material Volume 3 left
"for Volume 4" — the ण of रामेण (8.4.1–2, with 8.4.37 for रामान्) and the ष of रामेषु and करोषि
(8.3.59 under 8.3.55 and 8.3.57). From that lesson on, the bracket line under a quoted sūtra prints
the words the Aṣṭādhyāyī data file records as carried and the headings it stands under. The fourth
lesson is the Chapter 2 exam, one closed-book sitting of 45 minutes. The grammarians' line continues
with Kātyāyana and Patañjali, and the Mahābhāṣya's own statement of why grammar is studied. Every
quoted sūtra is printed as the data file gives it; every commentary line is "text of the GRETIL
e-text".

## Goals

- Take an owned word apart into base and affix, with its markers; read *sup* as a pratyāhāra
- Name the base aṅga, pada or bha, and say which rule each name sets off (the rājan example)
- Tell substitution, augment and deletion apart; define guṇa and vṛddhi as substitutes
- Read a sūtra whose words are carried down from the rules above, with its bracket line
- Derive, according to Pāṇini, the ण of रामेण and the ष of रामेषु and करोषि
- The 1.x errors corrected in the open (IR-016, IR-021, the rest of the p. 89 card, the rājan card of
  p. 98); the HISTORY of Kātyāyana and Patañjali hedged and keyed
- The Hindi parallels: गुण and वृद्धि as the names of joins in the Hindi school book and of vowels in
  Pāṇini; the ण and ष that tatsama words bring into Hindi already made

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Label the base, the affix and its markers in any owned noun form, and expand *sup*
- [ ] Say, according to Pāṇini, whether the base राजन् is strong, pada or bha before any case
      ending, and which rule each name sets off
- [ ] Sort the operations of a derivation into substitute, augment and deletion, and give the guṇa
      and vṛddhi of any इक् vowel
- [ ] Read a sūtra with its bracket line, say the completed rule, and choose its carried word from
      three offered
- [ ] Derive, according to Pāṇini, in table form, रामः, फलम्, नमः, राजभिः, राज्ञा, सूर्योदयः,
      अगच्छत्, रामेण, रामेषु and करोषि
- [ ] Say and write sentences about bases, affixes and rules in Sanskrit, joined, with the pause
      form beneath
- [ ] Read 16 ledger-new words (537 cumulative; Volume 4 so far: 29)
- [ ] Pass the Chapter 2 exam in one 45-minute sitting

## Assessment

Lesson 4 is the Chapter 2 exam: one closed-book sitting of 45 minutes, 2 of them for instructions;
Station 0 (about 8 minutes) runs before it and outside it. The booklet prints the chapter's toolbox
card (43 sūtras in number order, each with its text and one English line), open for tasks C–E. **A**
— base, affix and markers labelled in eight owned forms, the markers as lesson 1's ending-marker line
gives them (7 min; 16 points, pass 12). **B** — the base राजन् named strong, pada or bha before six
endings, with the reason (6 min; 12, pass 8). **C** — six operations, each said to be a substitute,
an augment or a deletion, with what in its rule shows it (8 min; 12, pass 8). **D** — "Derive,
according to Pāṇini, …": पुत्रेण, बालकेषु and राजभिः in full, and the ष of शृणोषि from the given stem
शृणो- (14 min; 20, pass 14). **E** — an unseen rule pair: 7.1.13 ङेर्यः after 7.1.12; the carried
word chosen from three offered, and रामाय derived from राम + ङे according to Pāṇini as far as the chapter's rules reach (the last step, 7.3.102, is printed for the learner: c06.l03) — 8 min; 8, pass 5.
Pass: every task at its pass mark, and the §19 quiz (after the sitting) at 7/10. The rubric weights
