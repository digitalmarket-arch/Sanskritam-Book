# Chapter 4 — कृत्-तद्धित-समासाः · Derivation & Compounds

Chapter 3 ran Pāṇini's machine on the verb. This chapter runs it on the words made from verbs and
from other words — the forms the learner has read since Volume 1 and Volume 3 promised to build. The
first lesson takes कृत "done" and गत "gone" apart: an affix after a root that is not a verb ending is
a kṛt (3.1.93); the niṣṭhā क्त and क्तवतु give the past participles (3.2.102, 1.1.26), and the क् of क्त
decides the root's shape — no guṇa in कृत [1.1.5], the nasal of गम् gone in गत [6.4.37], व् → उ in उक्त
[6.1.15, with 8.2.30]. Volume 3's R2 frame, मया कृतम्, is built, beside रामो वनं गतः and स कार्यं कृतवान्;
the present participles in -अत् and -मान stand in place of laṭ (3.2.124), and the 1.x "future
participle" of शप् + य is corrected by the participle inventory card. The second lesson builds the
absolutive, -त्वा for the earlier of two actions with one doer (3.4.21) and -य in a compound not headed by नञ्, as after a prefix (7.1.37,
6.4.38, 6.1.71); the infinitive -तुम् for the purpose of another action (3.3.10); and the agent
in -तृ — the k-marker's three jobs side by side, कृत्वा / कर्तुम्, गत्वा / गन्तुम्, उक्त्वा / वक्तुम्. Volume 3's R5
card closes, and the 1.x lesson on the gerund and the infinitive is restored with two of its lines
corrected. The third lesson is the taddhitas: affixes after a word (4.1.76) in the senses "the being
of", "who has it", "known in"; vṛddhi of the first vowel before a ñ- or ṇ-marked one (7.2.117; a k-marked one brings it too, by a rule in Teacher Notes), and
a final -अ or -इ dropped where the base is bha (6.4.148) — so एकता and बलवान् keep their base whole,
पक्षी and पाणिनीयम्, the volume's own name, lose the base's last vowel, and लौकिक loses it and takes vṛddhi. The fourth lesson shows the compound as a derivation: two
padas, one nominal base, the inner endings deleted (2.1.3–4, 1.2.46, 2.4.71), the first member still a
pada (राजपुरुषः); the upapada जलज, owned since Volume 1 (3.2.97, 6.4.143, 2.2.19); the samāsānta of
कपोतराजः and महाराजः [5.4.91, 6.3.46]; the kinds as Pāṇini files them; and one card on compound accent,
where the Mahābhāṣya's इन्द्रशत्रुः stands beside Whitney and the 1.x book's two identical glosses are
corrected. The fifth lesson is the Chapter 4 exam. The Hitopadeśa's story of the old tiger and the
traveller is read in the first two lessons and its next part, the trap sprung, unseen, in the exam; the serial "the monkey
and the crocodile, continued" takes the two friends out to sea. Every quoted sūtra is printed as the
data file gives it; every commentary line is "text of the GRETIL e-text".

## Goals

- Form the participles -त, -तवत्, -अत् and -मान of the owned verbs on the kṛt card, in the cells the
  lessons print, and use the -त in its two frames: the -त agreeing with the thing done, the doer in the
  instrumental (मया पुस्तकं पठितम्), and the -त agreeing with the doer (रामो वनं गतः)
- Join actions of one doer with -त्वा or -य, and form -तुम् and the agent in -तृ, saying what the
  k-marker of क्त and क्त्वा does that तुमुन् and तृच् lack
- Make the -ता/-त्व, -मत्/-वत् and -इन् words of owned bases, use -तर, -तम and यावत् … तावत्, read the rest
  of the card, and say when a taddhita brings vṛddhi
- Build a genitive tatpuruṣa from its vigraha, the upapada in -ज and the compounds in -राज and -सख, and file
  Volume 3's four kinds as Pāṇini files them
- Derive, according to Pāṇini, in table form, the chapter's participles, absolutives, taddhitas and
  compounds
- The 1.x errors corrected in the open (IR-024, the "future participle"; IR-017's second half, 8.2.39
  and 8.4.53; IR-026, the इन्द्रशत्रु glosses; the gloss of 7.1.37 and the use of 3.3.10); TRADITION and
  FACT side by side on the participle's two frames and on compound accent
- Read a Hitopadeśa story in two parts, the second unseen, printed joined with marginal glosses

      दाता

## Assessment

Lesson 5 is the Chapter 4 exam: one closed-book sitting of 45 minutes, 2 of them for instructions;
Station 0 (about 10 minutes: six compounds made and named, and पाणिनीयम् derived) runs before it and
outside it, so the room time is about 55 minutes. A separate sheet prints the toolbox card (131 sūtras
in number order, each with its text and the English line its lesson printed, no bracket line); it is
handed out when task C begins and collected when it ends. **A** — participles: the -त, the -तवत् and the
present participle of ten owned verbs (7 min; 30 marks, pass 22). **B** — six pairs of sentences
joined with an absolutive, four -तुम् forms and four agent nouns (in -तृ; 8 min; 20, pass 15). **C** —
three derivations, one each from lessons 2–4 (आगत्य, or गत्वा in its place; लौकिकम्; जलजम्), with the card
open (10 min; 18, pass 13). **D** — an unseen Hitopadeśa passage, the next part of the old tiger's story
(Book 1, kathā 1, the prose after 1.16), printed joined with nine glosses, and six questions on who did
what, and what first (11 min; 12, pass 8). **E** — a day in six sentences, with two absolutive chains,
an infinitive of purpose and two -त forms with the doer in the instrumental (7 min; 14, pass 10). Pass:
every task at its pass mark, and the §19 quiz (after the sitting) at 7/10. After the sitting,
unscored, the learner derives नयति again, the Chapter 3 item, and re-reads the Volume 3 thread verse
उद्यमेन हि सिध्यन्ति कार्याणि न मनोरथैः…, naming what Chapter 4 adds to it: सुप्तस्य is the -त of स्वप्, with
samprasāraṇa before the k-marked क्त. Retake after one week, with the alternative sets of the Teacher
Notes; the retake of task D reads an unseen passage of its own (Book 2, kathā 8, the prose after
2.123). Station 0 chain: l01 runs the Chapter 3 exam's +1-day booking and v4.c03.l05's +1-week booking;
l02 runs l01's +1-day item and the Chapter 3 exam's +1-week booking; each later lesson runs the lesson
before's +1-day item and the +1-week item of the lesson two before; l05 runs them before the sitting
and outside it; l04 books its +1-week item for v4.c05.l01; l05 books its +1-day item for v4.c05.l01 and
its +1-week item for v4.c05.l02.
