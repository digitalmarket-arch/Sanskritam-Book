## 1. Hook

You have been conjugating Sanskrit since Volume 1 — वदति arrived in Chapter 3, वदामि
in Chapter 5 — and this chapter finally told you what your mouth already knew: the
person lives inside the word. -मि is I, -सि is you, -ति is he, she, it. Today you
prove it in both directions: hear a form and name its speaker, be dealt a person and
build the form on a beat — then do the thing the whole course has been building
toward: tell the story of your own day, in your own first person, in Sanskrit.
Eleven ledger-new words this chapter; one pattern that turns them all into engines.
Time to drive.

## 4. Historical Context

ऐतिहासिकः सन्दर्भः — one paragraph, as in every review lesson.

Last chapter's exam closed the direction table; today's closes something larger: the
course's first formal grammar chapter, and with it the Volume 2 chapters 1–4 arc.
This chapter was also a structural repair. The 1.x curriculum introduced ten verb
tenses and moods at once, with zero prior conjugation practice — the audit's worst
sequencing finding. The 2.0 fix is this chapter's very shape: one tense, one number,
three endings, practiced to production depth before any survey arrives. The exam
honors the same honesty: it tests only the present singular you own.

> **[FACT · तथ्यम्]** A Sanskrit finite verb marks its person inside the word: the
> laṭ (present) singular endings are -मि (I), -सि (you), -ति (he/she/it). Dual and
> plural forms, other tenses, and the ātmanepada endings exist and are deferred —
> Volume 3 treats the laṭ fully; Volume 4 the system. [REF:whitney1889];
> [REF:goldman2019]

> **[FACT · तथ्यम्]** Spaced practice produces far more durable learning than the
> same practice massed together. [REF:cepeda2006]

## 5. Scientific Explanation

वैज्ञानिकं विवरणम् — why task C runs both directions, and why task E is the real
finish line.

> **[FACT · तथ्यम्]** Retrieval practice strengthens memory more than re-reading —
> the testing effect. [REF:roediger2006]

Recognizing गच्छामि as "I go" and *producing* गच्छामि when dealt अहम् + गच्छ- are
different skills stored at different strengths; a learner can hold the first without
the second. That is why task C is the chapter keystone and runs both directions —
produce the form from the person, name the person from the form — and why it is
timed at a beat: the pattern must arrive without translation, the automaticity that
makes real speech possible [REF:nation2013]. The sort (task D) checks the boundary
knowledge that keeps the pattern honest: what -ति does mark (person) and does not
(time). And task E moves the endings from exercise into *reference*: Sanskrit marks
the speaker inside the verb where English barely does (go/go/goes) and Hindi only
partly — मम दिनम् makes you use that machinery to say something true about yourself.
A pattern that has told your own story is a pattern you own.

## 6. Sanskrit Explanation

संस्कृतेन विवरणम् — the exam's three frames, fully readable:

> **अहम् गच्छामि। त्वम् गच्छसि। सः गच्छति।**
> (aham gacchāmi. tvam gacchasi. saḥ gacchati.) "I go. You go. He goes." — मैं जाता
> हूँ। तुम जाते हो। वह जाता है।

> **किम् त्वम् करोषि? — अहम् लिखामि। किम् सः करोति? — सः पठति।**
> (kim tvam karoṣi? — aham likhāmi. kim saḥ karoti? — saḥ paṭhati.) "What are you
> doing? — I am writing. What is he doing? — He is reading." — तुम क्या कर रहे हो? —
> मैं लिख रहा हूँ। वह क्या कर रहा है? — वह पढ़ रहा है।

> **श्वः अहम् गमिष्यामि??** न — the future is machinery the course has not
> delivered; -ति and its row are the present. The honest frame stays in the tense
> you own: **अद्य अहम् गच्छामि।** (adya aham gacchāmi.) "Today I go." — आज मैं जाता
> हूँ।

The exam's whole grammar fits in one line: the ending is the person. Everything else
— duals, plurals, tenses, the stems behind शृणोति, जानाति and करोति — is named,
deferred, and not tested.

## 7. Hindi Explanation

हिन्दी में समझिए

परीक्षा की विधि अब जानी-पहचानी है; आज की मुख्य बातें फिर तीन हैं।

पहली — **कार्य C ही इस अध्याय की कुंजी है।** बारह प्रश्न, दोनों दिशाएँ: कभी पुरुष
(अहम् / त्वम् / सः) और क्रिया दी जाएगी और आपको रूप बनाना है — पहले बोलकर, एक ताल के
भीतर, फिर लिखकर; कभी रूप दिया जाएगा और आपको पुरुष बताना है। नियमित क्रियाएँ ही बनानी
हैं (गच्छ-, खाद-, पिब-, पश्य-, लिख-, पठ-, वद-, हस-)। और याद रखिए: शृणोति, जानाति,
करोति के रूप जोड़कर नहीं बनते — ये पूरे शब्द याद किए गए हैं (शृणोमि शृणोषि शृणोति ·
जानामि जानासि जानाति · करोमि करोषि करोति); परीक्षा में ये पहचान और स्मरण के प्रश्न
हैं, निर्माण के नहीं। और 'अहम्' का रूप लम्बा होता है — गच्छामि, गच्छमि नहीं।

दूसरी — **मम दिनम्।** पिछले पाठ के गृहकार्य में आपने अपने दिन के छह 'अहम्' वाक्य
तैयार किए थे — वही आज की मुख्य प्रस्तुति हैं। पहले बोलिए (रिकॉर्ड कीजिए), फिर
लिखिए। फिर अर्जुनः वाली दिन-कथा सहज गति से दोबारा पढ़िए और समय दर्ज कीजिए। यह
पाठ्यक्रम की पहली ऐसी कथा है जो आपने स्वयं रची है — यही इस अध्याय की असली जीत है।
सरल और सही वाक्य, महत्वाकांक्षी और ग़लत से हमेशा बेहतर — भूतकाल, बहुवचन, नये ढाँचे
मत खींचिए; जो वर्तमान एकवचन आपका है, उसी में सच बोलिए।

तीसरी — **कार्ड-छँटाई।** नौ कथन हैं; हर कार्ड पर निर्णय (तथ्य या अस्वीकार) और एक
तर्क-पंक्ति। ईमानदारी वाले कार्ड ध्यान से: "-ति भविष्य बताता है" — अस्वीकार, क्योंकि
लट् वर्तमान है; काल आगे के खण्डों की बात है। "शृणोति सीधे -अति ढाँचे पर चलता है" —
अस्वीकार; वह अपना रूप अलग ढंग से बनाता है, और पाठ्यक्रम ने यह बात खुलकर कही है।
"गच्छति और अंग्रेज़ी go सजातीय हैं" — अस्वीकार; पाठ्यक्रम ने कहीं ऐसा दावा नहीं
किया — मिलती-जुलती ध्वनि रिश्तेदारी नहीं होती। और दो कार्ड इस पाठ्यक्रम के अपने
इतिहास के तथ्य हैं: 'अहम्' वाले रूप आप खण्ड 1 के अध्याय 5 से, और -ति वाले रूप
अध्याय 3 से बोलते आ रहे हैं।

|---|---|---|---|

The §19 quiz (10 questions) completes it. Pass = every task at partial-or-better

जानाति, करोति) appear only as whole-word rows: recognize, recall, recite — never
construct. Task E is the centerpiece and the arc's completion: six sentences about
your own real day, delivered aloud and written, followed by the day-passage at pace.
Chapters 1–4 of Volume 2 — courtesy, the world, numbers and time, and now verbs —
close here. Passing gates v2.c05 (संवादः — Daily Conversation), which is being
written next.

## 9. Pronunciation

उच्चारणम् — exam-day checks; reference values live in the lesson tables. Audio: 🔊
`planned:assets/audio/volume-2/v2c04l05-listening-test.mp3`,
`planned:assets/audio/volume-2/v2c04l05-dictation-set.mp3`.

**The ending is the exam.** -मि closes on the lips; -सि and -ति sit on the teeth —
dental /s/ and /t̪/, never English alveolar t. The listening test hangs entirely on
these tails: train the ear to catch the last syllable first.

**The 1sg stretch.** गच्छामि, पठामि, लिखामि — the आ is dīrgha, two full beats. A
clipped गच्छमि is a wrong form, not an accent.

**कृ's curl.** करोषि carries retroflex ष — tongue-tip back; गच्छसि stays dental स.
One habit each: the dictation checks the letter, the listening test the sound.

**The शृ- opening.** शृणोति and शृणोषि open on /ɕr̩/ — श plus the syllabic vowel ऋ,
then retroflex ण. No seam vowel: *śiru-* or *śri-* creeping in is the flagged habit.

**Warm-up before recording:** one triple slow (गच्छामि गच्छसि गच्छति), the three
whole-word rows once each, one मम दिनम् sentence at pace.

## 10. IPA

No new values. Exam spot-checks: the person lives in the final syllable — गच्छामि
/gɐt͡ɕt͡ɕʰɑːmi/ · गच्छसि /gɐt͡ɕt͡ɕʰɐsi/ · गच्छति /gɐt͡ɕt͡ɕʰɐt̪i/: the welded च्छ
/t͡ɕt͡ɕʰ/ holds across all three; only the tail changes, and the 1sg's /ɑː/ runs two
full beats. The endings are dental — -ति /t̪i/ and -सि /si/ on the teeth — but करोषि
/kɐɾoːʂi/ curls to retroflex /ʂ/, कृ's own habit. शृणोति /ɕr̩ɳoːt̪i/ hides syllabic
/r̩/ and retroflex /ɳ/ back to back. जानाति /d͡ʑɑːn̪ɑːt̪i/ carries two long /ɑː/ in
a row. करोति /kɐɾoːt̪i/ and शृणोति keep ओ a pure long /oː/ — never English "go"
[oʊ]. खादति /kʰɑːd̪ɐt̪i/ opens aspirated; पश्यति /pɐɕjɐt̪i/ welds श्य with no seam
vowel.

## 11. Mouth Position

मुखस्थितिः — the exam circuit, one check per task.

- **Task A (ears):** the question is always the same — whose verb is this? Catch the
  final syllable: lips closing = -मि = I; dental hiss = -सि = you; dental stop =
  -ति = the third person.
- **Task B (pen):** every sound gets its letter — च्छ welded as one conjunct, the ृ
  hook under शृ, ष in करोषि but स in गच्छसि, the long ा of the 1sg written because
  it is held.
- **Task C (mouth):** set the ending before the beat — know where the word will land
  before you launch it. A beat of silence is fine; a repaired ending counts as a
  slip.
- **Task D (head):** say the pattern aloud first ("the ending marks the person"),
  then judge the card against it — a card argued verdict-first is how overreach
  survives.
- **Task E (eyes then mouth):** plan the six engines before speaking — six verbs,
  all in the 1sg, all true of your day. Then let the sentences carry them.

## 13. Examples

उदाहरणानि — two worked model answers before you start.

**Model conjugation round (task C).** The deal: person card त्वम् + verb card लिख्-.
Within a beat: **लिखसि!** — then written, mātrā and all. Reverse direction: you hear
*pibāmi* — name the person: अहम्, "I". Now the traps. Dealt अहम् + गच्छ-: the answer
stretches — **गच्छामि**, never गच्छमि. Dealt त्वम् + शृ-: you do NOT build a form —
शृ- is not a regular stem; you recall the learned whole word **शृणोषि**. The exam
rewards the honest move every time: build only where the pattern lives, remember
where it does not.

**Model self-narration (task E).** Six engines, one true day: **प्रभातम् अस्ति। अहम्
विद्यालयम् गच्छामि। अहम् पुस्तकम् पठामि। अहम् अन्नम् खादामि। अहम् क्षीरम् पिबामि।

## 15. Grammar

व्याकरणम् — the chapter's rules restated on one page.

> **[FACT · तथ्यम्]** **The pattern:** the laṭ (present) singular marks its person in
> the ending — -मि "I" · -सि "you" · -ति "he/she/it". **The stretch:** the 1sg
> lengthens the stem vowel — गच्छ- gives गच्छामि, never गच्छमि. **The whole-word
> rows:** शृणोति, जानाति and करोति build their stems differently and are learned
> whole — शृणोमि शृणोषि शृणोति · जानामि जानासि जानाति · करोमि करोषि करोति; the ष of
> करोषि is कृ's own habit. **The trio:** अस्मि असि अस्ति — I am, you are, he/she/it
> is; the verbless frame (त्वम् छात्रः) remains legal and idiomatic. **The polite
> line:** भवान् takes the 3sg form, chunk-level. **The honesty lines:** present
> singular only — no duals, no plurals, no other tenses; -ति marks person, not time;
> the stem system is Volume 4's. [REF:whitney1889]; [REF:apte1890]

The regular bank, pattern-productive in both directions:

| Person | Ending | The regular bank (produce on demand) |
|---|---|---|
| अहम् (I) | -मि, with the stretch: -आमि | गच्छामि खादामि पिबामि पश्यामि लिखामि पठामि वदामि हसामि |
| त्वम् (you) | -सि | गच्छसि खादसि पिबसि पश्यसि लिखसि पठसि वदसि हससि |
| सः / सा (he / she) | -ति | गच्छति खादति पिबति पश्यति लिखति पठति वदति हसति |

The three irregular builders are tested as rows, not as products: recognize any cell,
recite any row — construct nothing.
