# Chapter 4 — क्रियाः · First Verbs

The course's first formal grammar — and the structural fix for the 1.x curriculum's
worst sequencing error. Learners have been *using* Sanskrit verbs since Volume 1
(वदति since Chapter 3, वदामि since Chapter 5): this chapter names the pattern they
already own — the present singular -मि / -सि / -ति — completes the 10-verb bank
(गच्छति, खादति, पिबति, पश्यति, शृणोति, लिखति, जानाति join पठति, वदति, हसति), delivers
असि to complete the identity trio, and puts it all to work in the doing-dialogue
(किम् करोषि?) and a full day-of-verbs passage. Stem honesty throughout: शृणोति,
जानाति and करोति build differently and are taught whole; tenses, duals, plurals and
the stem system wait for Volumes 3–4, and the chapter says so.

## Goals

- The 10-verb bank at production quality, sentence-embedded
- The person pattern named: -मि/-सि/-ति singular, both directions (produce and parse)
- असि; the doing-dialogue engine किम् करोषि? — अहम् पठामि।
- The day-of-verbs passage with the three-speed fluency protocol
- The gentle-ramp policy stated honestly: present singular only, practiced deeply

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Use all ten bank verbs in pause-form sentences with the noun stock
- [ ] Produce and parse the -मि/-सि/-ति forms of the regular bank on demand
- [ ] Run the doing-dialogue in both roles (किम् करोषि? / किम् सः करोति?)
- [ ] Read the day-of-verbs passage at pace and narrate their own day in 1sg
- [ ] Read ~12 ledger-new words (≈317 cumulative)
- [ ] Pass the verb exam (production conjugation both directions)
