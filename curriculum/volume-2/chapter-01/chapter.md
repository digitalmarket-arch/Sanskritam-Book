# Chapter 1 — अभिवादनम् · Greetings & Courtesy

Volume 2 opens where every conversation opens: the greeting. The learner already reads
and says नमस्ते, स्वागतम् and नमस्कारः; this chapter completes the courtesy kit — the
wellness exchange, the day-cycle greetings, thanks, apology, and farewell — and runs
Volume 2's new honesty axis at full strength: **every formula labeled classical or
modern-spoken**. धन्यवादः is taught proudly *and* labeled truthfully; स्वस्ति and
भद्रम् ते stand beside it wearing the classical label. Volume 1's exam interval lands
in lesson 1's Station 0, three artifacts in hand.

## Goals

- The complete courtesy kit: greet, welcome, ask after well-being, thank, apologize,
  wish the time of day, take leave
- The classical/modern usage labels applied word by word — the volume's register upgrade
- कथम् joins the question kit; न unlocks negation in pause form (न अस्ति)
- ञ's second working conjunct (ञ्ज in अञ्जलिः); the sandhi previews (पुनर्-, नास्ति,
  अनुगृहीतोऽस्मि) shown at the seams, taught in Volume 3
- Fluency maintenance: the V1 ladder re-runs on schedule inside this chapter

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Conduct a full courtesy exchange — greeting, wellness inquiry, thanks, apology,
  farewell — in pause form with correct register labels in mind
- [ ] Choose the right greeting for the time of day (सुप्रभातम् … शुभरात्रिः) and say
  which parts of the kit are classical idiom and which modern convention
- [ ] Use न for simple negation (न, न अस्ति, न कुशलम्)
- [ ] Read 23 ledger-new words — 26 headwords drilled (260 cumulative) — incl. अञ्जलिः, कुशलम्, धन्यवादः, स्वस्ति
- [ ] Improvise the lesson-5 role-card conversation and pass the chapter assessment
