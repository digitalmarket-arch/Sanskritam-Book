# Chapter 4 — सन्धिः · Sandhi in Use

Since the first chapter of Volume 1 the course has printed Sanskrit in pause form, every word as
it sounds before a pause, while the learner carried a handful of words that were plainly joined:
नमस्ते, नास्ति, स्वागतम्, कोऽपि, परमो धर्मः, पुनर्मिलामः, जगच्च, सर्वं कुशलम्, सत्यमेव. This chapter
opens them. It teaches the joins one kind at a time, and in every lesson the learner first takes
joined print apart and only then joins. It starts with vowels meeting: like vowels fusing
(नास्ति, विद्यालयः), the guṇa and vṛddhi vowels (परोपकारः, सदैव), the semivowel (स्वागतम्,
इत्यवदत्), the gap left at a word end (त आगच्छन्ति), the avagraha (तेऽपि) and the dual that never
joins (द्वे बालिके अत्र), with the seven sūtras that state them, among them 6.1.77 इको यणचि. It
then follows the visarga to the seam: नमस्ते is नमः + ते by 8.3.34 विसर्जनीयस्य सः, कोऽपि and परमो
धर्मः are the -अः column of one map, पुनर्मिलामः keeps the र् of an r-final, and सः drops its ः
before a consonant (6.1.132). The third teaching lesson brings the consonants: the anusvāra of
सर्वं कुशलम् by 8.3.23, the voiced षड् of षडादि- by 8.2.39, the च्च of जगच्च by 8.4.40, the doubled न्
of तस्मिन्नेव. The fourth lesson turns the whole process round: पदच्छेदः, a fixed order of checks
for taking any joined line back into its words, with the names संहिता and अवसानम् that 1.4.109 and
1.4.110 define and the Vedic tradition's पदपाठः, the honest finding that a split can be ambiguous and that printed editions
divide words differently, and the four changes inside words (the ṇ of रामेण, the ṣ of रामेषु, the
guṇa in गुरवे, भवति = भो + अति) named and left for Volume 4. From that lesson on, every taught text
in Volume 3 is printed joined, with its pause form beneath. The serial is re-read rather than
continued: our retelling of the doves and the mouse, after Hitopadeśa 1 (Mitralābha), comes back
lesson by lesson, joined so far — at its vowels, then its visargas, then every seam — and in the
fourth lesson the whole past-tense tale is printed joined over its pause form.

## Goals

- Vowel sandhi (स्वरसन्धिः): सवर्णदीर्घः, गुणः, वृद्धिः, यण्, अयादि, पूर्वरूपम्, and प्रगृह्यम्,
  the dual in -ई -ऊ -ए that never joins; sūtras 6.1.101, 6.1.87, 6.1.88, 6.1.77, 6.1.78, 6.1.109,
  1.1.11; the Hindi school names दीर्घ, गुण, वृद्धि, यण्, अयादि
- Visarga sandhi (विसर्गसन्धिः): the visarga map by the vowel before it and the sound after it;
  the r-finals पुनः, प्रातः, अन्तः; सः and एषः; भो राम; sūtras 8.3.34 and 6.1.132; the नमस्ते thread,
  step 2
- Consonant sandhi at word edges (व्यञ्जनसन्धिः): म् → anusvāra, voicing, त् + च/छ/ज, the doubled
  न्; the optional class nasal; sūtras 8.3.23, 8.2.39, 8.4.40; the Hindi anusvāra spelling set
  beside the Sanskrit class nasal
- पदच्छेदः: the reverse procedure as a written method, the reverse table, ambiguity; संहिता and
  अवसानम् by sūtras 1.4.109 and 1.4.110, and the tradition's पदपाठः; "editions vary"; the inner-word
  changes named, not taught
- Joined print: from v3.c04.l04 every taught text joined, the pause form beneath; in connected
  speech a seam is spoken without a pause, and a space in joined print is a word boundary, not a
  breath

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Take a joined line built from the taught seams back into its words, writing every candidate
      split where a seam is ambiguous (मूषक आगच्छति: मूषकः or मूषके, and the sense keeps मूषकः)
- [ ] Join words as joined print joins them — vowels, visarga, final consonants — with the spaces
      and the avagraha where they belong (सोऽपि जलमिच्छति; गुरुर्वदति; सत्यं वद)
- [ ] Read a joined story aloud with no pause at its seams, and write joined text from dictation
      with its pause form
- [ ] Explain why नमस्ते, नास्ति, विद्यालयः, परोपकारः and कोऽपि look as they do
- [ ] Name the fourteen sūtras of the chapter by number and gist, and say what संहिता, अवसानम्
      and पदपाठः are
- [ ] Read 21 ledger-new words (460 cumulative)
- [ ] Pass the chapter exam in one 45-minute sitting

## Assessment

Lesson 5 is the chapter exam, one 45-minute sitting (2 minutes of instructions, then five tasks):
A split sixteen joined phrases, each with its rule's name, with two volume-spiral questions (a
case and a verb form; 10 min); B join sixteen seams of mixed types (8 min); C read aloud the
Volume 2 crow story joined — the text the learner joined in lesson 4's homework, first seen here
in print — at natural pace (7 min); D dictation in joined form, five sentences played in a stated
order, then their pause form (8 min); E explain in three lines each नमस्ते, विद्यालयः, परोपकारः and
न कोऽपि लघुः, in 10 minutes. Pass: every task at its pass mark and the §19 quiz at 7/10. Station 0 (about
8 minutes) runs before the sitting and outside its 45 minutes, so the learner is in the room about
53 minutes. Station 0 chain: l01 runs v3.c03.l05's +1-day booking and v3.c03.l04's +1-week
booking; l02 runs l01's +1-day item and v3.c03.l05's +1-week item; from l03 on, each lesson runs
the previous lesson's +1-day item and the +1-week item of the lesson before that; l05 books the
+1-day item for v3.c05.l01 and the +1-week item for v3.c05.l02, and l04 books the +1-week item for
v3.c05.l01. From v3.c05.l01 the learner's own compositions are written joined, with their pause
form. Passing gates Chapter 5 (सर्वनाम-अव्यय-समासाः — Pronouns, Indeclinables, Compounds).
