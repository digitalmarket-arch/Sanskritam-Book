# Chapter 6 — सम्पूर्णपठनम् · Connected Reading

Five chapters have given the learner the cases, the verb of the present system, the joins between
words, the pronouns and the compounds. This chapter adds no new table: it puts all of them to work on
connected text, first the course's own and then the books'. The first lesson reads the two Volume 2
stories, the crow and the lion, joined as a book prints them, straight through and at three speeds,
and then a telling of the crow in the past that restores the 1.x reader's own details — तृष्णार्तः, the
stones in the plural, the moral बुद्धिर्बलाच्छ्रेष्ठा. It also gives the recognition card for the
absolutive (-त्वा, -य: "having …-ed"), which the chapter's Hitopadeśa and Pañcatantra passages use and Volume 4 will teach
to build; and it closes the thread-verse overlay for the benediction, every word now parsed. The second
lesson names the speech frame — X उवाच — "…", and "…" इति — and with it the serial ends: after five
chapters of our retelling, the learner reads eight of the Hitopadeśa's own sentences on the doves and
the mouse, with a bridge over the gaps, and checks every place where our retelling told it differently.
The third lesson opens the Pañcatantra's fourth book, the monkey and the crocodile, and the formula
with which its stories begin: अस्ति with a place, X नाम Y, and the storyteller's present with स्म — the
one sūtra the chapter quotes, 3.2.118 लट् स्मे. The fourth lesson teaches अन्वयः, putting a verse in
prose order, on three verses: विद्या ददाति विनयम्, the jackal's अयं निजः परो वेति … वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् read
with its speaker, and यस्य बुद्धिर्बलं तस्य, which says in a verse what the course's own moral बुद्धिः एव बलम् says in three words; the
Hitopadeśa's उद्यमेन हि सिध्यन्ति is read once more, with no gloss. The fifth lesson is the Volume 3 exam,
in two sittings; it gates Volume 4. Every authentic passage carries the source line "text of the GRETIL
e-text"; the Pañcatantra is cited by book and story.

## Goals

- Read joined narrative straight through: the three-speed protocol on joined print; every seam named by
  its Chapter 4 map
- The R5 recognition card: the absolutive in -त्वा and -य, "having …-ed", its doer the doer of the main
  verb or, in an "X-ed by Y" sentence, the instrumental (formation v4.c04)
- The speech frame: X उवाच / आह / अब्रवीत् / अवदत् — "…", and "…" इति X अवदत्; no join across the dash or
  the closing quotation mark; quoted words keep their persons
- The story-opening formula: अस्ति + a locative + the subject; X नाम Y; the storyteller's present + स्म
  — sūtra 3.2.118 लट् स्मे; कस्मिंश्चित् as a word of the formula
- अन्वयः in seven steps, on verses of the difficulty of Hitopadeśa 0.6; the śloka's four quarters of
  eight syllables by ear (metre v5.c04)
- Authentic text with honest source lines: the Hitopadeśa (Book 1, the doves and the mouse; introductory
  verse 6; 1.71 with its speaker), the Pañcatantra (Book 4, frame story; Book 1, the verse introducing
  the lion Bhāsuraka and the hare), the exam's unseen passage (Book 4)
- The Hindi parallels: -कर / -करके and the absolutive; कि before a quotation against इति after it; किसी गाँव
  में एक राजा रहता था against अस्ति कस्मिंश्चिद्ग्रामे … निवसति स्म; अन्वय

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Read a joined course-graded story aloud at natural pace with no pause at a seam, and split any
      joined word into its pause form with the rule's name
- [ ] Recognise an absolutive and read it as "having …-ed", finding its doer (recognition only)
- [ ] Say who speaks each line of a passage with speech frames, and move a line between X अवदत् — "…" and
      "…" इति X अवदत्
- [ ] Read the Hitopadeśa's eight sentences on the doves and the mouse, and say where our retelling
      departs from them
- [ ] Find the parts of a story-opening, and begin a story of one's own with अस्ति, X नाम Y and the
      present + स्म
- [ ] Put a verse into prose order (अन्वयः) and translate it, and give each verse's source and speaker as
      the lessons state them
- [ ] Read an unseen passage of the Pañcatantra, printed joined, with five glosses in 33 words, and answer
      questions on it in Sanskrit
- [ ] Read 20 ledger-new words (508 cumulative; Volume 3: 148)
- [ ] Pass the Volume 3 exam in two 45-minute sittings

## Assessment

Lesson 5 is the Volume 3 exam, in two 45-minute sittings, each with 2 minutes of instructions.
**Sitting 1 — reading and listening:** A, the lion-and-mouse dialogue heard joined at natural pace,
twice, and ten questions answered in Sanskrit, one of them a case question (9 min); B, dictation of five
joined sentences, played in order, written joined and then in pause form (8 min); C, an unseen
Pañcatantra passage (Book 4, frame story, the prose after the verses on the guest), printed joined
with five marginal glosses for 33 words: translate it and answer six questions in Sanskrit (26 min).
**Sitting 2 — production and knowledge:** D, twenty forms on demand — five noun cells, five verb cells,
four joins, four splits, two vigrahas — from the volume's productive cells only (14 min); E, a
composition: any Volume 3 story retold in ten sentences in the past, with two lines of speech closed by
इति and one relative sentence, written joined with its pause form (19 min), recorded aloud after the
sitting; F, an honesty sort of eight cards — verse sources and speakers, each restated in the lesson as
a cited HISTORY callout; the जयते line; नमस्ते = नमः + ते; मा with the imperative; the 1.x corrections;
the Hitopadeśa passage as a mosaic (10 min). No SYMBOL content is assessed. Pass: every task at its pass
mark and the §19 quiz at 7/10. Station 0 (about 8 minutes) runs before sitting 1, outside both sittings.
If a learner does not pass, the retake is the same unseen passage after two weeks, with new items for
the other tasks. Station 0 chain: l01 runs v3.c05.l05's +1-day booking and v3.c05.l04's +1-week booking;
l02 runs l01's +1-day item and v3.c05.l05's +1-week item; from l03 on, each lesson runs the previous
lesson's +1-day item and the +1-week item of the lesson before that; l04 books the +1-week item for
v4.c01.l01; l05 books the +1-day item for v4.c01.l01 and the +1-week item for v4.c01.l02, and closes the
"monthly until Volume 3 ends" task that Volume 2's exam booked. Passing gates Volume 4 (पाणिनीयम्).
