# Chapter 2 — सुबन्तविस्तारः · Nouns & Cases II

Chapter 1 gave the learner one of everything: one hunter, one dove, one net, every case of
the masculine and neuter a-stem in the singular. This chapter opens the rest of the noun.
It starts from many — the plural of रामः and फलम्, with the number words in the right gender
and the verb in -अन्ति beside a plural doer — and moves through the feminine (बालिका, with
नदी as its delta card, and the adjective that follows its noun's gender), the i- and u-stems
(अग्निः, गुरुः: गुरवे नमः at last explained), and the kinship and agent nouns in -ऋ (पिता,
माता, दाता), where पितरौ "the parents" brings in the dual as a card of three endings. Every
grammar lesson starts from a scene and from forms the learner already owns — मृगाः and
कार्याणि of the Hitopadeśa verse, सीता with no ः, कमले, गुरवे नमः, रात्रौ, मातृ and माता,
अयोगवाहौ — names the pattern, and lets the learner build the table before producing it. The
chapter closes with the roles map: the six kārakas with their usual cases, and the honest
finding that case and job part company — a dative with no receiver after नमः, an
instrumental of company with सह, the doer in the instrumental of मया कृतम्. Everything stays
in pause form (विराम-रूप), except the labelled quoted formula उक्तं च and owned joined items such as नमस्ते; joined text is Chapter 4's. The serial continues — our retelling
of the doves and the mouse, after Hitopadeśa 1 (Mitralābha): the hunter comes back with
grains and snares, the flock flies down, and the king of the doves is caught with his two
feet in the snare.

## Goals

- Table T2, the a-stem plural of रामः and फलम्, with देवानाम् beside रामाणाम्, and the plural
  askers and answers (के कान् कैः केभ्यः केषाम् केषु; ते तान् तैः तेभ्यः तेषाम् तेषु)
- Agreement: an a-adjective in the gender, number and case of its noun (प्रियः पुत्रः · प्रिया
  पुत्री · प्रियम् मित्रम्); a plural doer with its verb in -अन्ति
- The numerals 1–10 in the nominative and accusative, by gender (त्रयः, तिस्रः, त्रीणि — not
  "ततस्रः"); the rest of the numeral system is Volume 4's
- Table T3 (बालिका, with सा and ताः) and the नदी delta card; table T4 (अग्निः, गुरुः) and the
  feminine delta card (मतिः, धेनुः); table T5 (पिता, माता, दाता, स्वसा)
- The dual card: three forms for every noun and pronoun, with द्वौ/द्वे and उभौ; पितरौ
  "parents" by Pāṇini's sūtra 1.2.70, पिता मात्रा (pitā mātrā)
- Two recognition cards: the -इन् look-alike पक्षी (R1) and the -त participle of a transitive verb with its doer in
  the instrumental, मया कृतम् (R2)
- The roles map: the six kārakas and the places where case and job part company
- The Hindi bridge, stated exactly: the oblique plural (लड़कों), अच्छा/अच्छी/अच्छे against three
  genders, दो with a plural against the dual, the tatsama nominatives माता, पिता, दाता

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Talk about more than one in any case, with the verb's plural partner (पञ्च काकाः सन्ति;
      कपोताः तण्डुलान् खादन्ति)
- [ ] Describe people and things with adjectives that agree in gender, number and case, and
      count them with 1–10 in the right gender
- [ ] Decline the feminine, the i- and u-stems and the kinship words, producing the cells
      each lesson lists and recognising the rest
- [ ] Name pairs with the dual (द्वौ हस्तौ, द्वे नेत्रे, पितरौ)
- [ ] Say what job every noun in an owned sentence is doing, and show three places where
      case and job are not one-to-one
- [ ] Read 24 ledger-new words (411 cumulative)
- [ ] Pass the chapter exam in one 45-minute sitting

## Assessment

Lesson 5 is the chapter exam, one 45-minute sitting: A transformation (twelve items between
singular, dual and plural across the taught stems, 8 min); B agreement cloze (one adjective in
three genders against ten nouns, with numerals, 8 min); C the roles map (the job of every
noun in eight sentences, three of them not one-to-one, 8 min); D the thread verse उद्यमेन हि
सिध्यन्ति… parsed word by word, case and number (8 min); E family and home (six or more
recorded sentences with kinship words, one dual, three adjectives and two numerals, scored
on communication first and endings second, 10 min). Pass: every task at partial or better and
the §19 quiz at 7/10. Station 0 (about 8 minutes) runs before the sitting and outside its 45
minutes. Station 0 chain: l01 runs v3.c01.l05's +1-day booking and v3.c01.l04's +1-week
booking; l02 runs l01's +1-day item and v3.c01.l05's +1-week item; from l03 on, each lesson
runs the previous lesson's +1-day item and the +1-week item of the lesson before that; l05
books the +1-day item for v3.c03.l01 and the +1-week item for v3.c03.l02, and l04 books the
+1-week item for v3.c03.l01. Passing gates Chapter 3 (तिङन्तप्रवेशः — The Present-System Verb).
