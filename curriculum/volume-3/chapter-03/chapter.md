# Chapter 3 — तिङन्तप्रवेशः · The Present-System Verb

Volume 2 gave the learner the verb in the singular: गच्छामि, गच्छसि, गच्छति, and a handful of
whole words that were plainly something else — मिलामः, रोचते, क्षमस्व, वदतु, मुञ्च, आसीत्.
This chapter opens each of them. It starts with the present in all nine cells, three persons
times three numbers, so that पुनः मिलामः turns out to be "we meet", सन्ति and स्तः take their
places in a table, and two doers joined by च stand on one dual verb (रामः सीता च गच्छतः); the
stretch of गच्छामि gets its sūtra, 7.3.101 अतो दीर्घो यञि. It then brings in the second family
of endings, the ātmanepada (लभते, भाषते, मन्यते), where रोचते and सत्यमेव जयते belong, with the
passive -यते as a recognition card and an honest line on the Muṇḍaka's जयते. The story tense
follows: the imperfect, with the augment that stands after a prefix (आगच्छत्, प्राविशत्, न्यवसत्),
ऐच्छत् learned whole, and the speech words उवाच, अब्रवीत् and आह. The last teaching lesson is a
conversation: the imperative, built as a small delta on the imperfect and the present (3.4.85 लोटो लङ्वत्, 3.4.86
एरुः), polite requests with भवान् and the 3rd person, prohibition with मा, and the optative as a
card for rules and advice (छात्रः सत्यम् वदेत्). Every grammar lesson starts from a scene and from
forms the learner already owns, names the pattern, and lets the learner build the table before
producing it; the production target in every first-family table (present, imperfect, imperative) and in the
second-family present is the same — singular and plural in all persons, and the 3rd-person
dual — with the other duals shown and parsed; the second family beyond the present, अस् and कृ
beyond the present, and the optative card have listed cells. Everything stays in
pause form (विराम-रूप), except owned joined items in quoted verses and the one labelled R3
quotation from the Pañcatantra (तद्यथानुश्रूयते); joined text is Chapter 4's.
The serial continues — our retelling of the doves and the mouse, after Hitopadeśa 1
(Mitralābha): the king of the doves speaks in the net, the flock rises with the net to the
mouse's hole, the tale is told again in the past, and the king tells the mouse "don't cut my
snare first".

## Goals

- Table T6, first family: the present (लट्) of गच्छति in nine cells, with the subject pronouns
  वयम्, यूयम्, आवाम्, युवाम् as labelled words; अस् and कृ learned whole (स्मः, स्थ, सन्ति, स्तः;
  कुर्मः, कुर्वन्ति); sūtra 7.3.101 अतो दीर्घो यञि (ato dīrgho yañi)
- Table T6, second family: the ātmanepada present of लभते, each verb's family taken from the
  dictionary (Apte's P., A.); the R3 card (उच्यते, दृश्यते, श्रूयते, recognition only); the honest
  line on सत्यमेव जयते
- Table T7: the imperfect (लङ्), the augment after the prefix, ऐच्छत्, आसीत्; the chunks उवाच,
  अब्रवीत्, आह
- The imperative (लोट्) as a delta card on the imperfect (sūtras 3.4.85 लोटो लङ्वत्, 3.4.86 एरुः),
  with the second family's -स्व and -ताम् from the present; requests with भवान्; मा + imperative
- The optative (विधिलिङ्) card: the 3rd person for rules and advice; न + optative
- The R4 card: listed other cells of शृणोति, जानाति, गृह्णाति, स्वपिति, ददाति as whole words (3pl present, imperfect, 2sg imperative)
- The Hindi bridge, stated exactly: two numbers against three; मुझे … अच्छे लगते हैं beside मह्यम्
  … रोचन्ते; the Hindi ने against the Sanskrit imperfect's nominative doer; तू जा / तुम जाओ / आप
  जाइए against गच्छ / गच्छतु भवान्; चाहिए against the optative; मत against मा

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Use the present of every regular verb in the singular and plural of all persons and the
      3rd-person dual, and recognise the 1st- and 2nd-person duals (वयम् गच्छामः; तौ गच्छतः;
      आवाम् गच्छावः ◐)
- [ ] Use the second family of endings with the verbs that take it, and say what they like
      (फलानि मह्यम् रोचन्ते)
- [ ] Tell a short story in the past, with the augment in the right place and the speech words
      उवाच, अब्रवीत्, आह
- [ ] Ask, command, forbid and advise, politely with भवान् — for example भवान् उपविशतु; मा गच्छ;
      छात्रः सदा पठेत्
- [ ] Name the four lakāras of the present system and the two families of endings
- [ ] Read 28 ledger-new words (439 cumulative)
- [ ] Pass the chapter exam in one 45-minute sitting

## Assessment

Lesson 5 is the chapter exam, one 45-minute sitting: A a conjugation grid (verb + lakāra + person
+ number → the form, sixteen items, productive cells only: the present and the imperfect by the
chapter's formula, the imperative, the optative's 3rd person, the listed cells of अस्, कृ and the
second family, 10 min); B the tense switch (the Volume 2 crow story retold in the imperfect,
eight lines, 10 min); C a requests role-play (a guest at a teacher's home: four requests or
commands, two pieces of advice in the optative, one prohibition, polite भवान्, 10 min); D
listening (a short dialogue with plural and dual verbs, six questions, 8 min); E dictation (six
forms, 5 min). Pass: every task at partial or better and the §19 quiz at 7/10. Station 0 (about
8 minutes) runs before the sitting and outside its 45 minutes, so the learner is in the room
about 53 minutes. Station 0 chain: l01 runs v3.c02.l05's +1-day booking and v3.c02.l04's +1-week
booking; l02 runs l01's +1-day item and v3.c02.l05's +1-week item; from l03 on, each lesson runs
the previous lesson's +1-day item and the +1-week item of the lesson before that; l05 books the
+1-day item for v3.c04.l01 and the +1-week item for v3.c04.l02, and l04 books the +1-week item
for v3.c04.l01. Passing gates Chapter 4 (सन्धिः — Sandhi in Use).
