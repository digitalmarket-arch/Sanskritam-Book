# Chapter 5 — सर्वनाम-अव्यय-समासाः · Pronouns, Indeclinables, Compounds

Since Volume 1 the learner has carried pronoun forms as whole words: मम and तव, मह्यम् and
तुभ्यम्, सः and तस्य, कः and किम्, the ते of नमस्ते, the polite भवान् with its third-person verb, the
कश्चित् of the benediction. This chapter puts each of them in its table. The first lesson, a
conversation with a guest, opens the personal pronouns अहम् and त्वम्, with their four short forms
मे, ते, नः and वः, which never stand first in a sentence; 8.1.22 तेमयावेकवचनस्य names ते and मे, and
the नमस्ते thread closes with the learner's own three-line proof: नमः + ते, the short dative of त्वम्,
joined by 8.3.34. Beside them stands भवान्, "Your Honour", with its listed cells, its feminine भवती
and its plural भवन्तः. The second lesson assembles the pronominal declension of तद् from cells the
learner already owns and derives its family from it — एतद्, यद्, किम्, सर्व, अन्य, एक — with 1.1.27
सर्वादीनि सर्वनामानि to say why सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः says सर्वे; इदम् is its irregular member, and the
1.x rule "स् → त्" is corrected in the open: सः and तम् come from two stems. The third lesson turns
to the words that never change (1.1.37 स्वरादिनिपातमव्ययम्): the correlative grid, in which अत्र,
तत्र, यत्र, कुतः, ततः, कदा and इदानीम् turn out to be one system; the relative sentence यः …, सः …,
built clause for clause as Hindi builds जो …, वह …, with gender marked as well; the particles that never stand first; and the reference
card of the tradition's twenty-two upasargas (1.4.59 उपसर्गाः क्रियायोगे), with the 1.x list's errors
named. The fourth lesson teaches compounds through their विग्रहः in four kinds — द्वन्द्वः, तत्पुरुषः
with its subtypes, बहुव्रीहिः and अव्ययीभावः (2.2.29, 2.2.24) — and resolves the compounds of Volumes
1–2, from विद्यालयः and मातृभाषा to जलज and खग. The serial goes back to the day the doves flew with
the net and tells it again with names: our retelling, after Hitopadeśa 1 (Mitralābha), of the
dove-king चित्रग्रीवः, "whose neck is bright", and his friend the mouse हिरण्यकः. From the first lesson
on the learner's own compositions are written joined, with their pause form beneath.

## Goals

- अस्मद् and युष्मद् (Whitney §491): every singular cell and the plural nominative, accusative,
  instrumental and genitive productive; the short forms मे, ते, नः, वः and the rule that they never
  stand first; the duals and the other plural cells for recognition; sūtra 8.1.22; the नमस्ते thread,
  step 3
- भवत् "Your Honour" (Whitney §§452–456) in its listed cells, with भवती and भवन्तः and the third-person
  verb; सखा and सखे; स्वयम्; the R6 recognition card (-वान्, -मान्, महान्)
- The pronominal declension: तद् in three genders (T8), with एतद्, यद्, किम्, सर्व, अन्य, एक as Δ cards
  and इदम्'s key cells; the indefinites कश्चित्, काचित्, किञ्चित्, कोऽपि; sūtra 1.1.27; the suppletion
  correction (IR-025)
- Indeclinables: the correlative grid (-त्र, -तः, -दा, -था), the relative–correlative sentence (यः …
  सः; यत्र … तत्र; यदा … तदा; यथा … तथा), the particles च, वा, हि, तु, एव, इव, अपि, and अधः, उपरि,
  समीपे with the genitive; the upasarga card (22, IR-025); sūtras 1.1.37 and 1.4.59
- Compounds: the four rules (stem form first, ending last, the inner seam, the vigraha); द्वन्द्वः,
  तत्पुरुषः, कर्मधारयः, बहुव्रीहिः productive, द्विगुः, उपपद and अव्ययीभावः for recognition; sūtras 2.2.29
  and 2.2.24; the Hindi school grammar's names for the kinds of समास

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Talk about themselves and others with every productive cell of अहम् and त्वम्, using मे, ते, नः
      and वः where they may stand (फलं मे रोचते; never *मे फलं रोचते)
- [ ] Speak politely to a guest with भवान् or भवती and the third-person verb (भवान्कुत आगच्छति?
      भवत्याः पुत्री कुत्र?)
- [ ] Decline सः, सा, तत् and its family on demand, and use अयम्, इयम्, इदम् in their key cells
- [ ] Begin a story with कश्चित् … आसीत्, and say "someone, something, no one" with चित् and अपि
- [ ] Join two sentences with a relative and its correlative (यो मूषकः पाशान्कृन्तति स मम सखा;
      यत्र वृक्षास्तत्र कपोताः)
- [ ] Resolve a compound of the four kinds into its vigraha with the case of every member, name its
      kind, and coin transparent compounds from owned words
- [ ] Prove नमस्ते = नमः + ते in three lines, and name the six sūtras of the chapter by number and gist
- [ ] Write a dialogue joined, with its pause form beneath
- [ ] Read 28 ledger-new words (488 cumulative)
- [ ] Pass the chapter exam in one 45-minute sitting

## Assessment

Lesson 5 is the chapter exam, one 45-minute sitting (2 minutes of instructions, then five tasks): A
replace the noun named in each of eight sentences with the right pronoun cell, with one volume-spiral
case question (8 min); B join six pairs of sentences with a relative and its correlative, written joined
(7 min); C name the kind of ten compounds and give each vigraha with its cases (9 min); D read at sight a
new course-written passage on the serial's fifth episode, ten joined sentences, and answer ten questions
(10 min); E write a dialogue with a guest using भवान् or भवती, two relative sentences and two compounds
of one's own, joined with its pause form (9 min). Pass: every task at its pass mark and the §19 quiz at
7/10. Station 0 (about 15 minutes) runs before the sitting and outside its 45 minutes, so the learner is
in the room about 60 minutes. Station 0 chain: l01 runs v3.c04.l05's +1-day booking and v3.c04.l04's
+1-week booking; l02 runs l01's +1-day item and v3.c04.l05's +1-week item; from l03 on, each lesson runs
the previous lesson's +1-day item and the +1-week item of the lesson before that; l05 books the +1-day
item for v3.c06.l01 and the +1-week item for v3.c06.l02, and l04 books the +1-week item for v3.c06.l01.
Passing gates Chapter 6 (सम्पूर्णपठनम् — Connected Reading).
