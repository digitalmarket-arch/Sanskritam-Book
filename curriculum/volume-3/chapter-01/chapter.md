# Chapter 1 — सुबन्तप्रवेशः · Nouns & Cases I

For two volumes the learner has used Sanskrit nouns in more than one shape and been told,
honestly, that the reason would come later: विद्यालयः in one sentence and विद्यालयम् in
the next, सः मूषकम् गृह्णाति, घटे अल्पम् जलम्, वने, जाले, सिंहस्य उपरि, मह्यम् रोचते,
भोः राम. This chapter keeps those promises. It takes the masculine and neuter a-stem
(रामः, देवः, फलम्) through all eight cells of the singular, one function at a time, and
each case arrives as the answer to a question word: the doer and the done-to answer कः? and कम्?;
where and from where answer कुत्र? and कुतः?; with what and for whom answer केन? and कस्मै?;
whose answers कस्य?, and calling uses हे …!. Every grammar lesson starts from a scene and from forms the learner
already owns, names the pattern, and lets the learner build the table before producing it.
The tradition's names arrive with the jobs they name: प्रथमा … सप्तमी and सम्बोधनम् for the
cases; कर्ता, कर्म, करणम्, सम्प्रदानम्, अपादानम्, अधिकरणम् for the six roles Pāṇini calls
kārakas, with the honest note that the genitive and the vocative are not among them.
Everything stays singular and in pause form (विराम-रूप): the plural, the feminine and the
other stems are Chapter 2's, and joined text is Chapter 4's. The serial story of the
volume begins here — our retelling of the doves and the mouse, after Hitopadeśa 1
(Mitralābha) — with a hunter, a tree on the bank, one dove and a mouse in its hole.

## Goals

- Every case question about a masculine or neuter a-stem noun in the singular, asked and
  answered: कः कम् केन कस्मै कस्मात् कस्य कस्मिन्, with कुत्र and कुतः, and the answer words
  of सः: तम् तेन तस्मै तस्मात् तस्य तस्मिन्
- Table T1, the singular column of रामः and फलम्, with देवेन beside रामेण: the ending
  -एन, spelled -एण after र्, ऋ or ष् when nothing blocks it
- The eight case names and the six role names, each with an owned example; the genitive
  and the vocative as the two that do not hang on the verb
- The cells of अहम् and त्वम् each case pair needs: माम् त्वाम्, मया त्वया, मह्यम्
  तुभ्यम्, मम तव
- The Hindi bridge, stated exactly: को, से, के साथ, में/पर, का/के/की and हे
- Both Volume 2 stories read noun by noun, every case named, without a gloss

## Can-do statements

By the end of this chapter the learner can:

- [ ] Say who did what to whom, where, from where, with what and for whom in a pause-form
      sentence of a-stem nouns, from the endings alone and in any word order
- [ ] Produce any singular case of an owned a-stem masculine or neuter noun on demand,
      writing -एन or -एण correctly
- [ ] Ask the case questions and answer each in one word, or with तम्, तेन, तस्मै,
      तस्मात्, तस्य, तस्मिन्
- [ ] Hold a ten-turn conversation that asks whose things are and calls people with हे +
      the vocative (हे राम!, never *हे रामः!)
- [ ] Name each case by its traditional name and its job, and say, according to Pāṇini,
      which two cases are not kārakas
- [ ] Read 27 ledger-new words (387 cumulative)
- [ ] Pass the chapter exam in one 45-minute sitting

## Assessment

Lesson 5 is the chapter exam, one 45-minute sitting: A listening (a ten-sentence scene,
ten case questions answered in one word, 8 min); B dictation (eight inflected words and
two sentences, 7 min); C production grid (twelve noun-and-asker prompts, 8 min); D case
detective (every noun in both Volume 2 stories, its case and its job, 12 min); E scene
description (six recorded sentences about a picture, every case at least once, scored on
communication first and endings second, 10 min). Pass: every task at partial or better and
the §19 quiz at 7/10. Station 0 chain: l01 runs v2.c06.l05's +1-week booking; each lesson
runs the previous lesson's +1-day item and, from l03 on, the +1-week item of the lesson before that (l02 runs only l01's +1-day item); l05
books the +1-day item for v3.c02.l01 and the +1-week item for v3.c02.l02, and l04 books
the +1-week item for v3.c02.l01. Passing gates Chapter 2 (सुबन्तविस्तारः — Nouns & Cases
II).
